v0.8 pre-registration: amortized start predictor (main track, claims C1–C10)¶

Status: FROZEN on 2026-11-22, before any training. - The sha256 is in docs/research/v0.8-preregistration.sha256 and in the claims register's history. - The user reviews it at stop point 3. Any change before training is a dated amendment in docs/research/v0.8-preregistration-amendments.md. - Two choices were made at this freeze and await that review: - the 5-point superiority effect used for the power check (§9); - the Clopper–Pearson silent-miss bound (§8.1).

This is stop point 3 of docs/plans/neuralopt-v08-amortized-families.md. The document is frozen by recording its sha256 in docs/research/v0.8-preregistration.sha256 and in the claims register's history, before any training. After the freeze it changes only by a dated amendment in docs/research/v0.8-preregistration-amendments.md, never by editing this file (a test checks its hash). Exploratory results never change a claim's status.

Track v0.8-B (C11, C12) has its own pre-registration and is not covered here.

1. Sources and decisions¶

  • Plan: docs/plans/neuralopt-v08-amortized-families.md, §§2–11.
  • Claims register: docs/research/v0.8-claims.md (C1–C10; the criteria are copied in §8 below, with the amendments the user approved).
  • Gate F0:
  • results/f0-v0.8.0.json and the report docs/research/f0-v0.8.md;
  • per-instance validation data, results/f0-instances-v0.8.0.json, which reproduces the frozen F0 aggregates;
  • the pinned-thread probe re-time, results/f0-probe-pinned-v0.8.0.json.
  • User decisions after F0 (2026-11-22):
  • The default start (A0) is a required bar for every cost claim, next to the nearest-neighbour lookup (A3) and the better multistart arm.
  • Per-family expectations are stated before training (§3). A win on one family is never summarized as a general result; the joint-claim rule (§7) applies.
  • Compute:
    • one thread per process, pinned with taskset;
    • XLA intra-op threads 1; OMP_NUM_THREADS, OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS and MKL_NUM_THREADS set to 1;
    • the settings are recorded in every command.txt;
    • parallelism comes from separate processes;
    • every cap names its clock and is stated per run and in total (§10).
  • SciPy fairness: SciPy arms run with tolerances that let them use the full budget (ftol = gtol = 0, maxfun = budget/2). Default-tolerance runs are extra context only.
  • Power: checked with F0's validation spreads per family and claim (§9). Test sizes are raised where needed; criteria are never loosened.

4. Arms (frozen settings)¶

All arms share the same instances, float64, CPU, the counted unit budget (objective + gradient calls; objective and gradient reported separately) and the success tolerance f − f* ≤ 1e-6 + 1e-3·|f*|.

  • Reference optimum f*:
  • F0's protocol: SciPy L-BFGS-B multistart, 64 Sobol starts plus the truth, two seeds, 2,000 units each, ftol 1e-15 and gtol 1e-10.
  • It is updated, and the update reported, whenever any arm finds a lower value beyond tolerance.
  • Two-seed reproduction is reported. If it falls below 99% on a split, the reference budget is doubled for that split before any claim is evaluated.

5. The start predictor g_φ and its training (frozen)¶

  • Input: each observation is a token (t/10, standardized y, species one-hot, log σ, dose), where y is standardized with training-split statistics per species. Padding is masked.
  • Encoder:
  • a linear embedding to width 64;
  • 2 transformer blocks with 4 heads, masked self-attention and pre-layer-norm, GELU MLP of width 128;
  • masked mean pooling concatenated with attention pooling (one learned query).
  • Heads:
  • K = 8 candidates: a 2-layer MLP (width 128) outputs 8 × 4 values. Each is mapped into the optimization box by lo + (hi − lo)·(tanh(·) + 1)/2. The box is in the family's own coordinates: log parameters for the cascades.
  • Confidence: for each candidate, a 2-layer MLP (width 64) on the pooled embedding and the candidate gives a probability that owned L-BFGS from that candidate reaches success within B.
  • Losses (two candidate heads, compared in C7):
  • (R) regression: best-of-K squared error to the training-target optimum in box-normalized coordinates, min_k ‖u(θ̂_k) − u(θ*)‖².
  • (O) objective-based: best-of-K objective, min_k f(θ̂_k; c) / f_scale, with no unrolled steps (k = 0). f_scale is the training-split median of f*.
  • Training targets: owned multistart, 16 starts × 1,000 units (the F0 label procedure; label adequacy was 100% on validation in every family).
  • Optimizer:
  • Adam (optax), learning rate 1e-3, cosine-decayed to 1e-5 over 10,000 steps;
  • batch 64 training instances; gradient-norm clip 1.0; float64.
  • Validation loss is checked every 500 steps, and the best checkpoint is kept.
  • Confidence head (stage 2):
  • The candidate heads are frozen.
  • Each training instance's 8 candidates are refined with owned L-BFGS at budget B, giving success labels.
  • The confidence MLP is trained with binary cross-entropy: Adam 1e-3, 2,000 steps, batch 256.
  • Diversity check: the median pairwise box-normalized distance between candidates on validation is reported. Below 0.05 is reported as "collapsed". This changes nothing.
  • Seeds: 5 training seeds (0–4) per family and loss. Each instance's outcome is the mean over seeds; per-seed results and their spread are reported.
  • Validation-only choices, each made per family before the test is opened:
  • A4's loss: (R) or (O), whichever has the higher validation success at B; ties go to (R).
  • Fit check: f(θ̂*) ≤ τ_fit, where τ_fit is the 99th percentile of the reference optimum f* over validation instances.
  • Confidence threshold: τ_c is the smallest threshold for which A4F's validation silent-miss rate is ≤ 0.005 (half the C5 margin). Ties go to the lower fallback rate.
  • Checkpoints: schema neuralopt-start/v0.8-v1, which fails closed. Earlier schemas are untouched.

6. Metrics¶

  • Success, units and calls: as defined in the register's shared definitions. Objective and gradient calls are reported separately for every arm, along with the pinned-probe gradient/objective cost ratio.
  • B95: the smallest budget on the grid {25, 50, 100, 200, 400, 800} at which an arm reaches 95% success. Its interval comes from bootstrapping the success-versus-budget curve over instances.
  • Wall time:
  • warm, per instance, measured one instance at a time after compilation, on pinned single-thread processes on the same machine;
  • cold time (with compilation) and batched time are reported separately.
  • g_φ time is included in A4's warm time.
  • Parameter error: only on instances that the identifiability check (Fisher condition ≤ 1e6 at θ*) marks as identified. Non-identified instances are counted.

7. Statistics and the joint-claim rule¶

  • Intervals:
  • paired over test instances, percentile bootstrap, 95% two-sided, 10,000 resamples, bootstrap seed 0;
  • learned arms: instance outcomes are seed means, and seeds are resampled with instances (two-level bootstrap).
  • Per-family decisions: each claim's criterion (§8) is applied separately per family. The register records one status per family. Within a family, claims that require several conditions (C2, C3, C4, C5) are intersection-union tests: every condition must hold, and no further correction is applied.
  • Joint claims across families:
  • Any statement made jointly about several families, such as "the learned start helps on the families tested", "on k of 3 families" or a pooled number, uses Holm–Bonferroni over the families it covers, at a family-wise one-sided level of 2.5%.
  • Each family's p-value is the largest one-sided bootstrap p-value among the conditions of its criterion.
  • Without that correction, results are reported side by side, and no general claim is made from a single family's win.

8. Criteria (copied from the register; amendments marked [amended])¶

8.1 Amendments (decided by the user after F0; they apply where they differ from §8.2)¶

  • C2 [amended, decision 1]: supported only if, on both axes, A4 beats each required bar:
  • the bars are A0, A3, and the better of A2 and A2o (chosen per axis);
  • on units: the 95% interval of B95_A4 / B95_bar lies entirely below 1;
  • on wall time: the 95% interval of warm time A4 / warm time of the bar lies entirely below 1.
  • Not supported if A4 never reaches 95% within 800 units, or if any ratio's interval lies entirely above 1.
  • Otherwise inconclusive.
  • C4 [amended, decision 1]: N* is computed against each bar, with each bar's own offline cost:
  • A0: none;
  • better multistart: none;
  • A3: its label and index cost, so N* versus A3 uses the incremental offline cost.
  • Supported only if t_bar − t_A4 > 0, with an interval lower bound > 0, for every bar.
  • Not supported ("no break-even") if any bar's interval upper bound is ≤ 0.
  • Otherwise inconclusive.
  • C3: the cost axis already compares against A3. A0 is added as a reported reference on that axis; it is not part of C3's criterion, because C2 now carries the A0 bar.
  • C5: the comparator A2 runs with the full-budget SciPy settings (decision 4).
  • C5 silent-miss bound [amended at freeze; stricter than the register, so it loosens nothing]: the silent-miss rate's 95% upper bound is the exact Clopper–Pearson bound, not a percentile bootstrap. A bootstrap of a rate near zero degenerates: zero observed misses give [0, 0] at any sample size.
  • The user may revert this, by a dated amendment, before any training.
  • Matched budget B is fixed per family as in §2. Every other criterion is unchanged.

8.2 Register text at the freeze (verbatim, C1–C10)¶

C1. Basin landing¶

  • Wording: Learned start + L-BFGS reaches the reference optimum on more instances than default start + L-BFGS at the same budget.
  • Test: A4 versus A0 at budget B, owned L-BFGS in both.
  • Criterion (proposed): supported if the paired success-rate difference A4 − A0 has a 95% interval whose lower bound is > 0.
  • Not supported if the upper bound is ≤ 0.
  • Inconclusive otherwise.
  • Split: in-distribution test.
  • Status: unverified

C2. Cost versus multistart¶

  • Wording: At a matched success rate, learned start + L-BFGS uses fewer units and less wall time than multistart L-BFGS-B.
  • Test: A4 versus the better of SciPy multistart (A2) and owned multistart (A2o).
  • "Better" is decided separately on each axis: the lower B95 for units, the lower warm time for wall-clock.
  • The matched rate is 95% success. Compared: B95 in units, and warm wall time per instance at that budget.
  • Cold and batched times are reported as well. Objective and gradient calls are reported separately.
  • Criterion (proposed): supported only if it holds on both axes:
  • the 95% interval of B95_A4 / min(B95_A2, B95_A2o) lies entirely below 1;
  • the interval of warm time A4 / (the better arm's warm time) lies entirely below 1.
  • Not supported if A4 never reaches 95% within 800 units, or if either ratio's interval lies entirely above 1.
  • Otherwise inconclusive.
  • Split: in-distribution test.
  • Status: unverified

C3. The neural part earns its place¶

  • Wording: The learned start beats the nearest-neighbour warm start, on both success and cost.
  • Test: A4 versus A3, both refined with owned L-BFGS.
  • Criterion (proposed), a superiority claim: supported only if A4 is better than A3 on at least one axis (a paired interval excluding zero, in A4's favour) and non-inferior on the other.
  • Success axis:
    • better: the interval of the success difference A4 − A3 at B has lower bound > 0;
    • non-inferior: lower bound ≥ −0.02 (−2 percentage points).
  • Cost axis:
    • better: the interval of B95_A4 / B95_A3 has upper bound < 1;
    • non-inferior: upper bound ≤ 1.02. This is the −2 percentage-point margin expressed as a relative cost margin.
  • Not supported if neither axis shows A4 better (each interval includes zero or favours A3), or if A4 is inferior on either axis.
  • If not supported, this entry must state plainly: "a nearest-neighbour lookup does as well as (or better than) the learned start on this family."
  • Split: in-distribution test.
  • Status: unverified

C4. Break-even¶

  • Wording: The number of instances after which training plus target-solving cost is recovered.
  • Test: N* = T_offline / (t_generic − t_amortized).
  • T_offline is the measured wall time of:
    • simulating the training set;
    • solving all training targets (the reference multistart);
    • training, including compilation.
  • t is the mean warm time to success at the matched success rate of C2.
  • t_generic is the better of A2 and A2o; t_amortized is A4, with the g_φ forward pass included.
  • Batched and per-instance variants are both reported.
  • Criterion (proposed):
  • supported if t_generic − t_amortized > 0 with an interval whose lower bound is > 0. Then N* is reported with its interval and the measurement protocol: hardware, float64, timing method.
  • Not supported ("no break-even") if the interval's upper bound is ≤ 0.
  • Inconclusive otherwise.
  • Split: in-distribution test (timings); offline costs from the actual runs.
  • Status: unverified

C5. Safety: no silently missed optima¶

  • Wording: With the confidence check and fallback, the success rate is not worse than multistart L-BFGS-B.
  • Test: A4F (fallback included in its units) versus A2, at the larger of the two arms' B95 budgets.
  • Silent miss (definition): the fallback was not triggered, and the final fit is worse than the reference optimum by more than the success tolerance, i.e. f(θ̂) − f* > ε_abs + ε_rel·|f*|.
  • The silent-miss rate is the share of all instances that are silent misses.
  • Criterion (proposed): supported if both hold:
  • the interval of the success difference A4F − A2 has lower bound ≥ −0.01;
  • the silent-miss rate's interval has upper bound ≤ 0.01.
  • Not supported if either bound fails decisively: the success upper bound is < −0.01, or the silent-miss lower bound is > 0.01.
  • Otherwise inconclusive.
  • Split: in-distribution test; also reported on OOD (C8).
  • Status: unverified

C6. Informed versus random multistart¶

  • Wording: K informed starts beat K random multistarts at equal K and budget.
  • Test: A4K versus A2o at the same K and the same total units.
  • K = 4 is primary.
  • K = 1, 2 and 8 are exploratory: reported, but they do not decide the claim.
  • Criterion (proposed): supported if, at K = 4, the interval of the success difference A4K − A2o has lower bound > 0.
  • Not supported if the upper bound is ≤ 0. Otherwise inconclusive.
  • Split: in-distribution test.
  • Status: unverified

C7. Objective-based versus regression training (secondary)¶

  • Wording: Objective-based training beats regression on families with non-identifiable directions.
  • Test: A4 trained with the objective-based loss versus A4 trained with the regression loss: same architecture, data, seeds and budget.
  • Applies only to families/variants that F0's identifiability check marks as having non-identified directions (expected: 2b). Fisher-information conditioning above a pre-registered threshold, or flat profiles.
  • Criterion (proposed): supported if the success difference (objective − regression) at B has lower bound > 0.
  • Not supported if the upper bound is ≤ 0. Otherwise inconclusive.
  • If no family qualifies, the status is inconclusive (not applicable).
  • Split: in-distribution test.
  • Status: unverified

C8. Generalization and OOD detection¶

  • Wording: Performance on OOD splits is reported, and the confidence score flags OOD instances.
  • Test:
  • All arms on each OOD split (noise, parameter range, sampling times, dose), with the same metrics as the in-distribution test. This part is reported regardless of outcome.
  • The AUROC of 1 − confidence for separating OOD from in-distribution test instances.
  • The detection rate (the share of OOD instances below τ_c) at the validation-tuned threshold.
  • Criterion (proposed): supported if, on every OOD split:
  • part 1 is fully reported;
  • the AUROC interval has lower bound > 0.5;
  • the detection rate is reported.
  • Not supported if any split's AUROC upper bound is ≤ 0.5.
  • Otherwise inconclusive.
  • Split: the OOD test splits, plus the in-distribution test as the negative class.
  • Status: unverified

C9. Precision is not required¶

  • Wording: Basin landing does not require precise predictions. The relation between prediction error and success is reported.
  • Test:
  • Prediction error: the distance from the start to θ* in normalized log-parameter coordinates.
  • Reported: success as a function of error decile for A4, plus a logistic fit, with the NN start (A3) on the same axes.
  • Criterion (proposed): supported if the success rate in A4's highest prediction-error quartile is ≥ 0.8 × A4's overall success rate, with the interval of the ratio having lower bound ≥ 0.8.
  • Not supported if the ratio interval's upper bound is < 0.8.
  • Otherwise inconclusive.
  • Split: in-distribution test.
  • Status: unverified

C10. The forward pass is cheap¶

  • Wording: The forward pass costs negligible time compared with refinement.
  • Test: warm and cold g_φ time versus the warm and cold owned-L-BFGS refinement time per instance, single and batched, measured in the same process.
  • Criterion (proposed): supported if the warm ratio (forward / refinement) has an interval whose upper bound is < 0.05.
  • Cold times (first call, compilation included) are reported for both the forward pass and the refinement, single and batched, but do not decide the claim.
  • Not supported if the warm ratio's lower bound is > 0.05. Otherwise inconclusive.
  • Split: in-distribution test.
  • Status: unverified

9. Power check (F0 validation spreads)¶

Data: - results/f0-instances-v0.8.0.json: F0 recomputed per instance with the unchanged F0 harness, under pinned single threads. It reproduces every frozen F0 success rate exactly, for all arms and families. - results/power-v0.8.0.json: written by scripts/power_v0_8.py.

Rule: - Each comparison is powered at 80% at the two-sided 95% level for its effect: n = ((z_0.975 + z_0.8)·SD/δ)². - SD is the validation spread of the paired difference between two real start strategies at the family's matched budget B: - C1, C3 and C7: A3 − A0; - C6: A3 − A2o (K = 4); - C5: A0 − A2 (full-budget SciPy). - No learned arm exists yet. A paired binary difference with true mean δ has a spread of at least sqrt(δ(1 − δ)), so the proxy SD is floored there. - Effects: - superiority: 5 percentage points (C1, C3-success, C6, C7); - the criteria's own margins at a true difference of 0: C3 non-inferiority 2 points; C5 success 1 point; - C5's silent-miss bound: the exact Clopper–Pearson upper bound ≤ 1%, at an assumed true rate of 0.25%. This is half the validation target that sets τ_c. - Test size per family: the largest requirement among the family's primary claims (§3), rounded up to 100, with a minimum of 200. Secondary claims are reported with the power they have at that size.

10. Compute (pinned threads; clocks named)¶

Clock and pinning: - All caps are on the process-CPU clock (time.process_time, summed over a run's processes). - Every process is pinned to one core with taskset, with every thread count set to 1 (§1). So CPU time ≤ wall time per process, and parallelism comes only from separate processes. - The settings, the CPU affinity and the cap are written to each run's command.txt.

Re-timed probe: results/f0-probe-pinned-v0.8.0.json, pinned threads. - Per-call timings match the original probe within about 10%. - Gradient/objective cost ratios: 2.5 (Family 1), 10.8 (2a), 11.0 (2b). - F0 is projected at 2.63 CPU-h, versus 2.57 multi-threaded. - The F0 per-instance extraction (pinned) actually took 0.11, 0.57 and 0.67 CPU-h.

11. Outcome reporting¶

  • Every claim gets a per-family status: supported, not supported, inconclusive, or "not tested (reason)". Each status comes with its intervals and the numbers behind them.
  • A family's results are reported against its §3 expectation. A null result on Family 2a is the expected outcome.
  • If C3 is not supported on a family, the register states: "a nearest-neighbour lookup does as well as (or better than) the learned start on this family."
  • Negative results, cost regressions and failed checks are reported as findings.
