v0.8 pre-registration: amendments¶

Dated amendments to the frozen docs/research/v0.8-preregistration.md (sha256 in docs/research/v0.8-preregistration.sha256). The frozen file is never edited.

Each amendment states: - the date; - whether any training or test data had been seen; - what changes and why; - that no criterion is loosened, or else the user's explicit approval.

Amendment 1 (2026-11-23): C2 is decided on a continuous cost to success¶

  • Data seen: validation only. No training has run and no test or OOD split has been opened.
  • Decided by: the user.
  • Reason: on Family 2b the default start sits at 94.5% success at 400 units, so "units to 95%" on the coarse grid flips between 400, 800 and "none" when the instances are resampled. The power check showed that C2's units axis would be undecidable at any test size. C2's wall axis was defined at that same budget.

Per-instance cost to success¶

  • Single-start arms (A0, A3, A4): c_i is the unit count at which the arm's best-so-far value first meets the success tolerance. The accounting is F0's: an objective call per trial, a gradient on acceptance; a SciPy call is 2 units. The run is capped at 800 units, and c_i = ∞ (censored) if it never succeeds within 800.
  • Multistart arms (A2 with full-budget SciPy; A2o owned): sequential restarts over the 16 Sobol starts (seed 3), in order.
  • Start k begins at k·p and may use min(p, 800 − k·p) units. c_i is the unit of the first success, or ∞.
  • This is the continuous form of F0's "K starts with B/K units each": success within budget b equals F0's K = b/p configuration.

  • The per-start cap p was chosen on validation from {25, 50, 100, 200, 400, 800}, maximizing the mean area defined next. Frozen values:

    Family 1 Family 2a Family 2b
    A2o 100 400 400
    A2 (full-budget SciPy) 100 200 400

Summary: the area under the success-versus-budget curve¶

  • Grid: G = {10, 20, …, 800} units, 80 budgets.
  • Per-instance area: a_i = (1/80)·#{b ∈ G : c_i ≤ b}, in [0, 1]. It is 0 if the instance never succeeds, which is how censoring is handled.
  • The mean of a_i is the arm's average success rate over the budget grid, equivalently 1 − its restricted mean cost to success divided by 800, on the grid.

C2, amended¶

  • Units axis:
  • for each required bar (A0, A3, and the better multistart arm), take the paired differences d_i = a_i(A4) − a_i(bar);
  • A4's a_i is the mean over its 5 training seeds;
  • intervals are the pre-registration's §7 rule: paired, 95%, two-level bootstrap, 10,000 resamples.
  • Wall axis:
  • per-instance warm wall cost to success t_i = t_forward + κ_arm·c_i for A4 (with κ_A4 the owned-L-BFGS warm seconds per unit), and t_i = κ_bar·c_i for a bar; ∞ if censored;
  • κ_arm is each arm's warm seconds per unit, and t_forward the g_φ forward time. Both are medians over 50 validation instances, run one at a time on pinned single threads after compilation, and frozen before the test is opened.
  • The time grid for comparing with a bar is {j·T/80 : j = 1..80}, T = 800·κ_bar. Per-instance time-area and paired differences are computed as on the units axis.
  • The better multistart arm is, separately on each axis, whichever of A2 and A2o has the higher mean area on the test split. Taking the stronger of the two on test data makes the bar harder, never easier.
  • Supported only if, on both axes and for every bar, the 95% interval of the mean difference has lower bound > 0.
  • Not supported if, on either axis, any bar's interval has upper bound < 0.
  • Inconclusive otherwise.
  • Reported only, not deciding: units to 95% (B95) on the F0 grid with its bootstrap interval; objective and gradient calls separately; cold and batched times.

C4, consequential change¶

  • C4's t ("mean warm time to success at the matched success rate of C2") referred to the B95 match that no longer decides C2.
  • t is now each arm's restricted mean warm time to success: the mean of min(t_i, T), with T as in C2's wall axis for that bar.
  • The rest of C4, including its criterion, is unchanged.

Power check for the amended C2 (validation)¶

  • Proxies: the paired area differences between real start strategies. The effect is 0.05, five points of average success over the grid, about 40 units of restricted mean cost.
  • results/c2-validation-v0.8.0.json and results/power-c2-v0.8.0.json, written by scripts/c2_validation_v0_8.py and scripts/power_c2_v0_8.py:

Loosening check¶

  • A4 must still beat every bar, on both axes.
  • Censored instances count as failures (area 0).
  • The better multistart bar is chosen on test data in its own favour.

Nothing is loosened.

Amendment 2 (2026-11-23): C3's cost axis uses the same continuous measure¶

  • Data seen: validation only, before any training.
  • Made by: the agent, applying amendment 1's reason. The user may revoke it before the test is opened; C3's cost axis would then revert to the frozen B95 ratio.
  • Reason: C3's cost axis compares B95_A4 / B95_A3. On Family 1, where C3 is primary, A3 never reaches 95% within 800 units (validation: 74.5%). B95_A3 is undefined there, so the axis cannot be decided.
  • Change: C3's cost axis uses the paired per-instance area difference d_i = a_i(A4) − a_i(A3) from amendment 1.
  • Better: the 95% interval's lower bound > 0.
  • Non-inferior: lower bound ≥ −0.02. That is 2 points of average success over the budget grid, the same size as the success axis's margin.
  • The success axis and the rest of C3 are unchanged.
  • Power (Family 1, proxy A0 − A3, SD 0.56):
  • better at 5 points: 982 instances;
  • non-inferiority at 2 points: 6,130 instances.
  • Both fit within the frozen 7,000.
  • Loosening check: the B95 rule could not be decided on Family 1, and the new margin matches the success axis. Nothing is loosened.

Review record (2026-11-23)¶

The user reviewed the two choices made at the freeze and kept both: 1. the 5-point superiority effect used for the power check (frozen §9); 2. the exact Clopper–Pearson upper bound for C5's silent-miss rate (frozen §8.1).
