3. Conclusion (exploratory)¶

Depth and width are not the binding limit. - Width improves imitation fit: relative A error falls from 0.70 to 0.33–0.35, and the direction cosine rises from 0.92 to 0.96–0.97. Depth adds little. - But no fitted configuration performs well in the shared loop. Every one is worse than BB on validation (−1.79 to −2.70, against −3.32), and worse than the frozen meta-trained model (−3.3 to −3.9). - Even direction cosines of 0.96–0.97 with the oracle do not reproduce the oracle's −15.99. That points to the loop's sensitivity to small metric errors and to the shift between oracle-visited and self-visited states. Neither cause was isolated.

There is no head saddle. - Gradients reach the A and C heads from the frozen init, and A/C magnitudes grow 3–30× in 200 updates. - They stay 20–150× below the oracle's median ‖A*‖_F of 4.5. - The A/C gradient is only about 0.14–0.19 of the δ/σ gradient, and every update is dominated by δ: total norms of 135–370 are clipped to 1.0. - With init scale 1.0, A and C do not collapse, but their share stays at 0.44–0.57, and δ moves most (|δ| 0.3 to 1.1–1.6).

Overall, the failure is undetermined, leaning towards optimization and objective signal rather than representation. The meta-gradient mostly drives the scalar step (δ/σ). Larger networks fit the oracle better without performing better in the loop. Which change would let A and C be learned (the loss, the gradient balance or clipping, on-policy imitation, or more updates) was not tested. Any such change belongs to a future v0.8 pre-registration, not to v0.7.

Decision and next work¶

  • By plan gate G7, the trajectory-curvature line is not expanded further. The validation-tuned fixed diagonal and the BB step remain the reference geometries, and no v0.7 learned geometry becomes a default.
  • The next work is learned guidance for certified global search. This was set by the user on 2026-11-22. In this repository it connects to plan gate G6 (optional feasible-incumbent candidate injection into certified search), which the v0.6 plan deferred until learned geometry showed value. Certification itself remains independent and model-free.
  • Any changed model or training setup for the Gram line, including the post-mortem ideas above, needs a new pre-registration (v0.8). It is not added to v0.7.
  • Update, 2026-11-22: direction changed. After the bullet above was written, the user chose amortized optimization over problem families ("train an optimizer for your problem family": a learned start or step policy, refined by NeuralOpt's own loop) as the next direction. Learned guidance for certified global search (plan gate G6) is deferred, not cancelled. The earlier bullet is kept as written for the record; the v0.8 plan will be written in docs/plans/neuralopt-v08-amortized-families.md.

Development history: model, gates, loop rule and masks (before the pre-registration)¶

The model (B1; opt-in, schema neuralopt-policy/v0.7-gram-v1)¶

Source: neuralopt.gram. It runs only through minimize(..., model_kind="gram", candidate_method="preconditioned", preconditioned_engine="compiled").

  • Tokens. There is one token per stored secant pair plus one for the current gradient: K+1 tokens, independent of the dimension d. Every input is a normalized inner product among the columns of S, Y and g_u, or a log norm ratio, mask, pair age, rejection count, last rejected step length, or observed/predicted change ratio of the last rejected trial. Rejected trials never form secant pairs.
  • Outputs. Masked attention produces A and C (K×K), δ and σ. They define P = τ[e^δ (I − ŜAŶᵀ)(I − ŶAᵀŜᵀ) + ŜCCᵀŜᵀ] + ετI, applied without forming the matrix in O(Kd).
  • τ = sᵀs/sᵀy of the newest valid pair when sᵀy > 0, otherwise fallback_length/‖g_u‖.
  • The step is u' = clip(u − shrink·σ·P g_u, −1, 1), with the same acceptance and accounting as the shared loop.
  • What P is. P is symmetric positive definite for every A and C, and it is an inverse metric (P y ≈ s). Its directional structure is confined to span(Ŝ, Ŷ); on the complement it is isotropic.

Gate 1: algebraic, float64 (tests/test_gram.py)¶

All checks pass.

  • (a) The model can express L-BFGS. With every valid pair having sᵀy > 0, set
  • τe^δ = γ = sᵀy/yᵀy of the newest pair,
  • A = R⁻ᵀ and C = R⁻ᵀD^{1/2}/√τ, with Rᵢⱼ = sᵢᵀyⱼ for i ≤ j (chronological) and D = diag(sᵢᵀyᵢ),
  • converted to normalized columns as Â = D_s A D_y and Ĉ = D_s C.

Then P g equals the L-BFGS two-loop direction with H₀ = γI to within 1e-10. This holds for d/K = 10/4, 10/6, 16/8 and 5/5, and P y = s holds on the newest pair. The coefficients lie inside the model's output ranges. This is the compact representation of Byrd, Nocedal & Schnabel (1994); the independent derivation review found no error. - (b) SPD. P is symmetric positive definite for random A and C (minimum eigenvalue at least ετ). The matrix-free product equals the dense matrix. Masked pairs, rows and columns, contribute nothing. - (c) Invariance. Tokens are invariant to rotation of u and to positive rescaling of the objective. Whole trajectories rotate with a rotated quadratic: a ±1000 cube at the origin with an interior optimum, so clipping is never active. Trajectories are unchanged by rescaling the objective, which rescales τ, while σ is dimensionless.

Criterion, stated before the first run: for both rotated κ = 100 and κ = 1000, the oracle-BFGS arm's median log10 regret must be within one decade of SciPy L-BFGS-B on the same tasks.

Conditions: d ∈ {7, 10, 16}, 4 tasks per d, gate-only seeds 6000+, budget 100 objective+gradient units, float64. The oracle sets A, C and δ to the BFGS values and σ = 1. It lives only in the harness, scripts/gram_reference.py and scripts/run_gram_gates_v0_7.py. The harness copy of the loop reproduces the package's compiled Gram loop to 1e-10 over 37 iterations; this is checked before any oracle number is reported.

Further measurements: - Reached 1e-6 regret: at κ=100, oracle 12/12 and L-BFGS-B 12/12; at κ=1000, oracle 5/12 and L-BFGS-B 8/12. - Acceptance: the oracle accepted 89% of trials at κ=100 and 93% at κ=1000, so rejections are rare. - Conditioning: the oracle's P reached a median maximum condition number of 149 at κ=100 and 1,363 at κ=1000. The Gram parameterization therefore expresses at least 100× anisotropy. - Coefficient sizes: the oracle coefficients never left the model's output ranges. The maximum |Â| was 13.4 and |Ĉ| 7.8, against a bound of 100; |δ| reached 4.84, against a bound of 5. Clipping to the model's ranges changes nothing.

Reading¶

  • κ=100. Both arms reach 1e-6 on every task. The 2.2-decade gap lies at relative regret around 1e-14 to 1e-16, the float64 roundoff zone. Under the pre-stated rule this still counts as a failure, and it is reported as one.
  • κ=1000. The 3.3-decade gap is substantive.
  • Where the limit is. The same BFGS directions, run through this loop rule, fall well short of L-BFGS-B at equal budget. The loop accepts any decrease with a unit step and only halves after a rejection. There is no sufficient-decrease or curvature (Wolfe) condition and no step lengthening.
  • Untested explanations: the accepted steps may produce poorer secant pairs than Wolfe line-search steps do, and K = 8 against SciPy's default memory of 10 may also matter.
  • Rejection signal under clipping. With a non-diagonal P, a clipped step can ascend for every shrink factor. This is a loop-rule property, shared with v0.6, and it makes the rejection signal ambiguous near active bounds. These gate tasks have interior optima.

Gate 2 as written, and the amended criterion¶

  • As written, gate 2 FAILED and stays failed.
  • At κ=100 both arms are at the float64 floor, and the original criterion had no regret floor.
  • For the reruns below, the user stated an amended criterion before any rerun was made:
  • per task, log10 regret below −12 counts as −12, so regrets below 1e-12 are equal;
  • an arm passes iff its floored median is within one decade of SciPy L-BFGS-B's floored median, at both κ=100 and κ=1000.

Loop-rule ablation (results/loop-rule-ablation-v0.7.0.json)¶

Setup¶

  • Same gate tasks as gate 2, float64, budget 100 objective+gradient units.
  • Every arm steps through neuralopt.loop_rules.accept_step, so all arms share one step-acceptance rule. Only the BFGS oracle's direction lives in the harness.
  • The harness outer loop reproduces the package's compiled engine (fixed geometry) to 1e-10 under each rule before any number is reported.
  • "Initial unchanged" means the model's own step was accepted without correction.

The three rules¶

  • halving (v0.6): one trial per iteration; any decrease is accepted; a rejection halves the next trial.
  • armijo: sufficient decrease with interpolating backtracking. Each trial costs 1 objective unit, and the accepted point costs 1 gradient unit.
  • wolfe: strong Wolfe conditions with bracketing and zoom, lengthening up to 10×. Each trial costs 2 units.

Reading¶

  • The step-acceptance rule is not the bottleneck for good directions.
  • Under all three rules the oracle's own step is accepted unchanged 86–93% of the time at κ=1000.
  • Changing the rule moves the oracle by at most 0.5 decades.
  • For the no-learning baselines the rule matters, in the other direction. At κ=100, τ-only does best under halving (−5.23, against −3.52 and −3.32).
  • History length matters a lot.
  • SciPy loses 2.8 decades at κ=1000 when its memory drops from 10 to 8.
  • At matched memory 8, every oracle loop is within 0.3–0.5 decades of SciPy.
  • At K=10, a gap of 2.1–2.6 decades to SciPy (maxcor 10) remains under every rule. The cause is not identified.
  • The amended criterion fails. Every arm is at the floor at κ=100, but at κ=1000 none comes within one decade of SciPy L-BFGS-B at its default memory.
  • Uncertainty. Medians over 12 tasks are coarse, and no paired intervals were computed for this ablation.

Mask diagnostic (oracle K=10, halving; results/mask-diagnostic-v0.7.0.json)¶

The decision rule was stated before running: relaxed masks count as meaningful only if they reduce the κ=1000 median gap to SciPy (maxcor 10) by at least one decade. - Masking is rare. Under the current thresholds (step 1e-7, relative gradient change 1e-6), only the step test fires: on 3.4% of filled pairs at κ=1000 and 18% at κ=100. The gradient-change and nonfinite tests never fire. On average 8.6 of 10 pairs are valid at κ=1000. - Relaxed masks change nothing at κ=1000. With float64 thresholds (1e-12 and 1e-10), or masking only nonfinite pairs and pairs with sᵀy ≤ 0, the oracle median stays at −6.19. The reduction is 0.0 decades, so not meaningful. - At κ=100 relaxed masks help, moving the median from −14.47 to −15.98, but that is at the float64 floor. - Paired over tasks at κ=1000, oracle − SciPy is mean +0.86 [+0.18, +1.60] (raw) and +0.69 [+0.08, +1.41] (floored at −12), with median +0.06. - The gap sits at d=10: the oracle reaches about −6 and SciPy −8.5 to −9.8. At d=7 both are at the floor; at d=16 both are near −5. - The cause is unexplained.

Step-acceptance rules (scope)¶

loop_rule is a step-acceptance rule in the sense of CLAUDE.md, not a solver. - It decides whether, and how far, to move along the model's proposed displacement. - The model's step is always the first trial. - No curvature is built: there is no BFGS update and no classical solver. - It is opt-in, on the compiled engine only (loop_rule="halving" | "armijo" | "wolfe", default "halving"). - The Python loop, the v0.6 rule and all frozen results are unchanged.

tf-h8 anisotropy cap (the frozen v0.6 checkpoints)¶

  • With geometry_bound = 10 and lowrank_scale = 0.5, the diagonal ranges over [0.1, 10] and λmax(UUᵀ) < 0.25.
  • So the cap is 100× along the coordinate axes and about 3.5× off-axis.
  • Along tf-h8 runs (3 seeds × rotated κ=1000 × d = 7/10/16), the bound cond(P) ≤ (diag_max + ‖U‖_F²)/diag_min reached a maximum of 19.9 and a median of 3.3.

Review findings and dispositions¶

Source: curvature-reviewer, 2026-11-22.

  • Fixed:
  • Gram diagnostics now record τ and σ in their own fields, with a test.
  • The oracle arm now reports coefficient sizes, and a correctly clipped bounded-oracle arm was added.
  • The gate (a) test now checks that the coefficients lie inside the model's ranges.
  • The compiled engine's docstring now describes the Gram rule.
  • A separate fix from this round: the compiled engine casts the budget fraction to the parameters' dtype, which removes a future-JAX type error for float32 checkpoints on float64 problems.
  • Open, for the next design decision:
  • The pair mask uses a max-norm, so it is not exactly rotation-invariant near its 1e-7 threshold.
  • The default ε is 1e-12, which provides no positive-definiteness floor in float32.
  • A near-noise newest pair can make τ very large in float32.
  • The oracle handles all-valid histories; masking is tested only for the oldest slots.
  • Any change to the token layout must bump GRAM_SCHEMA.
  • The clip-induced ascent described under "Rejection signal under clipping".

Corrections¶

These are deviations from the frozen pre-registration, or details it did not fix. The frozen file is never edited.

  1. Training group sampling (fixed before any training). The groups (quadratic-d4/8/12 with 28 tasks each, Rastrigin-d4/8/12 with 4 each) are cycled in proportion to their size, 7:1, so every training task is equally likely. Uniform cycling would have put 50% of updates on Rastrigin.
  2. Reporting-only harness additions (committed before Stage 1 training): per-run loss curves and CPU time; checkpoint sha256 in the results; exploratory per-family tables; error-status counts. No decision, threshold or claim changed.
  3. The first Stage 1 launch failed before training: /usr/bin/time is not installed. Nothing was trained; the relaunch timed itself with date plus each run's process CPU time.
  4. The Stage 2 evaluation re-evaluates the Stage 1 arms on the same frozen tasks (deterministic). It reproduces the Stage 1 decision exactly: −0.139 [−0.334, +0.044].
  5. Untrained-weights diagnostic and post-mortem (added after Stage 2, at the user's request): both are exploratory, not pre-registered, and feed no decision. They used training and validation tasks only, except that the untrained diagnostic used the already-built test tasks.
  6. CPU accounting: process CPU time includes XLA helper threads, so it is about twice the per-run wall time. The probe's estimate was a single-process wall-time projection.
  7. A test was changed before training so that it does not build test-split tasks. The equal-spectrum property is now checked on seeds outside all splits.

Limitations¶

  • Statistics. Five training seeds make the seed level of the bootstrap coarse. Medians and floored means at −12 hide differences below 1e-12.
  • Scope. Exact gradients and float64 only; noise-aware pair reliability is untested. The test dimensions are 7, 16 and 32 for a model trained at 4, 8 and 12.
  • The oracle's gap to SciPy at d = 10 (gate tasks) remains unexplained. At the test dimensions the oracle is close to SciPy.
  • BB here means τ-only (sᵀs/sᵀy of the newest valid pair) inside the frozen halving loop. It is not SciPy and not v0.6's harness-only BB variant.
  • tf-h8 is context only: a v0.6 history-v1 model with float32 checkpoints, run here through the compiled engine on float64 problems.
  • Untested hypotheses. Why A and C were not learned (their training signal, the loss, the 2,000 updates or the model size) is not established. Neither is why the trained models leave the Rastrigin and Ackley basins. Neither was investigated, and nothing was tuned after the results.
