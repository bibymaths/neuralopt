v0.5 transferable learned geometry¶

Result¶

The prespecified hypothesis was not supported. Under matched objective budgets, the corrected transformer did not outperform a validation-tuned fixed diagonal on the 30-instance held-out set. This negative result is retained because it identifies a representation limitation that lower training loss obscures.

Reconciliation of v0.4¶

Re-evaluating the original checkpoints, seeds, and configurations reproduced every deterministic reported metric exactly; timings were intentionally excluded from this equality check. The machine-readable comparison is results/geometry-diagnostics-v0.5.0.json.

The two reported overdamped FP-minus-regret values came from different checkpoint generations, not different evaluation populations. Commit c9ee283 reported -0.089 [-0.181, -0.008] on ten pairs. After review fixes removed sampled quadratic-noise variance from the FP loss and matched BAOAB initial momentum to inference, commit 0ecc22f retrained the checkpoints and reported -0.048 [-0.105, -0.006] on the same ten pairs. The corrected value supersedes the pre-review result.

Diagnosis and minimal corrections¶

The v0.4 feature map normalized the gradient direction and discarded its norm: scaling a gradient by 1000 changed the predicted diagonal by only 1.2e-7. Consequently the model could not transfer across objective scales. v0.5 reuses the same input width but replaces the constant feature, when enabled, with a bounded log gradient-scale feature. The corresponding checkpoint changes its diagonal by 0.131 in the same diagnostic.

The positive-definite geometry remains

\[A_\theta=\operatorname{diag}(d_\theta)+U_\theta U_\theta^\top.\]

For new checkpoints, d is bounded symmetrically in log space and U is dimension/rank normalized and bounded with tanh. This controls condition and low-rank energy without changing legacy checkpoint behavior. Median learned condition was 1.23 and the largest per-run median was 3.09. Diagonal-only, low-rank-only, and full heads are explicit ablations. Temperature and friction are sampled from their inference ranges, and multiple explicit random keys can be averaged for pathwise stochastic meta-gradients.

Four-sample gradients did not reduce observed gradient-norm variability in this small diagnostic (coefficient of variation 2.39, versus 1.78 with one sample). This is evidence against assuming that simple key averaging solves the variance problem; it also mixes task heterogeneity and is not an estimator of within-task variance alone. The fixed-compute loss remains constant and has no gradient. Incumbent min decisions are piecewise differentiable; gradients flow through the selected branch but not through the discrete selection boundary.

The remaining dominant limitation is information, not positive definiteness. At a step, the transformer sees position, one gradient, bounds, incumbent, and scale. A single gradient cannot identify rotated curvature. Episode updates add history dependence but do not expose curvature history as a sufficient statistic. The model therefore improves easy isotropic cases while regressing on the ill-conditioned and rotated families. No architectural expansion was made because the held-out result did not justify it.

Protocol¶

  • Unchanged small transformer: width 16, four heads, rank two, one layer.
  • Ten training and ten validation tasks; 30 final tasks with disjoint seeds and entirely unseen dimensions 7, 8, and 10.
  • Isotropic, condition-1000, rotated condition-100, Rastrigin, and Ackley families; dimensions 2--10; objective scales 0.2--5 and coordinate scales 0.5--2.
  • Training ranges: temperature 0.25--1, friction 0.5--2; horizons 2/4/8; 144 updates. A 36-update full checkpoint tests training budget.
  • Fixed scalar selected only on validation from 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, and 4. Selected values were 0.25 for overdamped and 2 for BAOAB.
  • Final budget 100 with paired seeds and 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples.

Reproduce training and evaluation with uv run python scripts/run_transfer_geometry_v0_5.py; pass --reuse-checkpoints to re-evaluate saved checkpoints. Reproduce the diagnosis with uv run python scripts/run_geometry_diagnostics_v0_5.py.

Held-out comparisons¶

Differences below are paired mean log1p(regret) relative to tuned fixed geometry; negative is better.

Overdamped learned diagonal 3.282 +0.075 [-0.064, 0.246] 13
Overdamped learned full 3.295 +0.088 [0.021, 0.164] 10

Overdamped learned low rank 3.522 +0.315 [0.065, 0.609] 7

BAOAB learned diagonal 2.922 +0.053 [-0.247, 0.353] 14
BAOAB learned full 2.927 +0.058 [-0.204, 0.322] 13

The full overdamped model at 36 updates was already worse than fixed (+0.103 [-0.058, 0.329]); 144 updates narrowed uncertainty but remained worse. This rejects insufficient training budget as the sole explanation. FP regularization did not establish an improvement over fixed or regret-only training.

Family results explain the aggregate. Overdamped full geometry improved the isotropic mean (0.686 versus 0.745), but regressed on ill-conditioned quadratics (7.031 versus 6.835) and rotated quadratics (4.052 versus 3.954). Low-rank-only was especially weak on those families. BAOAB learned geometry helped isotropic tasks but lost on ill-conditioned, rotated, and Rastrigin tasks. Thus no favorable aggregate is used to conceal family regressions.

Frozen-checkpoint transfer at temperatures 0.125 and 1.5 and budgets 50 and 150 also found no credible improvement. Overdamped point estimates were worse in all 12 learned comparisons; at temperature 1.5 and budget 150 all three learned intervals excluded zero on the harmful side. BAOAB intervals crossed zero in all eight comparisons. These settings were not used to retune the fixed baseline.

All methods used the same objective budget and recorded objective/gradient evaluations, stochastic integration steps, success, time-to-target, incumbent and candidate improvements, displacement, condition, finite rate, first-call time, and warm time in results/transfer-geometry-v0.5.0.json. Training time and checkpoint size are recorded there as well. Timings are single-host descriptive measurements because compilation caches make process-order comparisons imperfect. Existing direct and identity-HMC results remain available in results/hamiltonian-smoke-v0.2.0.json; they are contextual baselines rather than part of this geometry ablation because they use a different transition and historical task population. They were not pooled with the matched v0.5 pairs.

Mathematical scope and limitations¶

Overdamped noise uses covariance 2 T h A; BAOAB uses the exact OU covariance T(1-exp(-2 gamma h)) M. Tests cover positive definiteness, low-rank algebra, fluctuation--dissipation scaling, stochastic reproducibility, pathwise gradient flow, weak generator identities, and Gaussian equilibrium. The FP term is skipped entirely when disabled, avoiding overflow in an inactive diagnostic.

The learned policy is episode-frozen only within each rollout segment and is history-dependent between segments. It is therefore an adaptive optimization policy, not fixed Gibbs-invariant dynamics. Euler and BAOAB also have finite-step bias; no exact finite-step stationarity is claimed. Certification and independent certificate replay are unchanged, and neural or stochastic candidates never replace a rigorous certificate.

Only one meta-training seed and 30 final instances were affordable in this study. Per-family samples are six, so family means are diagnostic rather than high-powered. Objective closures are compiled independently, and transformer overhead remains material. A future test of learned curvature should first add a compact, explicitly justified secant/history statistic and preregister a larger multi-seed evaluation; it should not enlarge the transformer by default.
