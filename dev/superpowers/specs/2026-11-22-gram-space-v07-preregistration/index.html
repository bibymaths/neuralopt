NeuralOpt v0.7 Track B: Gram-space history model, pre-registration¶

Status: FROZEN on 2026-11-22. The approved draft was revised as instructed. This file's sha256 is recorded in the commit that follows it and in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-gram-space-v07-preregistration.sha256. No training had been run when it was frozen. Any later deviation goes under "Corrections" in the results report (docs/research/gram-space-v0.7.md), never into this file. Literature: docs/research/trajectory-curvature-literature.md (v0.7 additions, verified against OpenAlex).

1. Question¶

Can a small model that sees only rotation-invariant statistics of K = 10 actually evaluated secant pairs produce an inverse metric P that beats an untrained Barzilai–Borwein (BB) step on unseen rotated and ill-conditioned problems? The comparison uses equal objective+gradient budgets and one frozen step-acceptance rule.

2. Evidence this design rests on (measured before freezing)¶

  • Gate 1, algebraic (passed). With the BFGS coefficients (A = R⁻ᵀ, C = R⁻ᵀD^{1/2}/√τ, τe^δ = γ), P g equals the L-BFGS two-loop direction to within 1e-10, and the coefficients lie inside the model's output ranges. P is SPD for every A and C, the tokens are invariant, and trajectories are rotation-equivariant and invariant to objective scale.
  • Anisotropy of at least 100× is demonstrated. In gate 2 the oracle's P reached condition numbers of 149 at κ = 100 and 1,363 at κ = 1000 (medians of per-run maxima). The frozen v0.6 tf-h8 geometry is capped at about 3.5× off-axis (at most 19.9 measured).
  • Gate 2. It FAILED as written. The amended criterion (regret floor 1e-12; within one decade of SciPy maxcor 10) also fails at κ = 1000.
  • Loop-rule ablation. The step-acceptance rule moves the oracle by at most 0.5 decades.
  • Frozen loop: halving, K = 10.
  • Halving is the strongest rule for the untrained BB-like baseline, so the bar is not weakened.
  • Mask diagnostic. The decision rule was stated before running.
  • Relaxed masks reduce the κ = 1000 median gap by 0.0 decades, so they are not meaningful for the gap.
  • Paired oracle − SciPy at κ = 1000 (d = 7/10/16): mean +0.86 [+0.18, +1.60], median +0.06.
  • The gap sits at d = 10 and is unexplained.

3. Capacity ceiling (stated, not a target)¶

The oracle uses BFGS coefficients and σ = 1 in the frozen loop with history-v2 masks (harness only). The table gives median log10 regret and the number of tasks reaching 1e-6.

Tasks κ = 100 κ = 1000 Paired oracle − SciPy maxcor 10
gate tasks, d = 7/10/16 (12 per κ) −15.98 (12/12) −6.19 (7/12) κ=1000: +0.86 [+0.18, +1.60] (gap at d = 10, unexplained)
validation seeds 8000+, d = 32 (4 per κ) −9.30 (4/4) −5.07 (0/4) κ=100: −0.34 [−0.47, −0.14]; κ=1000: −0.02 [−0.23, +0.20]

The d = 32 values come from results/oracle-ceiling-d32-v0.7.0.json, measured on validation seeds, not test tasks. At d = 32 the oracle matches or beats SciPy L-BFGS-B. The learned model is not expected to exceed this ceiling.

4. Frozen design¶

  • Engine and rule. Compiled engine, float64 throughout (features, training, evaluation). loop_rule="halving": one trial; any finite decrease is accepted; a rejection halves the next trial. No Armijo test, and no BB-based base step inside the model: BB enters only as the τ scale and as a reference arm.
  • Model. model_kind="gram", K = 10 pairs, width 32, 4 heads, 2 layers (14,080 parameters). Output ranges: |A|, |C| < 100; |δ| < 5; σ ∈ [1/4, 4]; ε_rel = 1e-12; fallback length 0.1.
  • history-v2 masks (approved). Schema neuralopt-policy/v0.7-gram-v2, with the thresholds stored in its config.
  • Thresholds: max|s| > 1e-12 and ‖y‖ > 1e-10·max‖g‖, plus finiteness.
  • Reasons: the protocol is float64; the masks are neutral at κ = 1000, reach the floor at κ = 100, and were chosen before training.
  • Coverage. The tokens carry ŜᵀŜ (rank and conditioning of the observed span); P is isotropic on the unobserved complement by construction.
  • Noise-aware reliability is untested: no noisy-gradient family is in this round.
  • Rejection information. The rejection count, the last rejected step length and the ratio of observed to predicted change. Rejected trials never form pairs.
  • No-history control (history_masked=True). Every pair is masked, so the model sees only the gradient token (rejection information and budget fraction) and τ uses its fallback.
  • The training unroll is matched to the evaluation budget of 100 units.
  • The training loop is the inference loop under lax.scan: the same accept_step halving rule, the same budget accounting (start costs 2 units; a trial costs 1, plus 1 if it improved), and the same stopping condition (used + 2 ≤ 100 and a nonzero gradient). Inactive scan steps are masked.
  • The scan length is the maximum number of iterations at budget 100, which is 97. The budget-fraction input is 1 − used/100, as in inference.
  • A parity test against the compiled engine is required before any training: trajectory, history and counts must match to 1e-10.
  • Training settings.
  • Starts are random in the box.
  • Loss: mean over unit horizons {25, 50, 100} of log(normalized incumbent regret + 1e-12), plus 0.1 × the mean over active steps of candidate log-regret.
  • The history buffer (S, Y, f) is treated as observed data: stop_gradient applies in both the tokens and the metric. The meta-gradient flows through the current position and ∇f, so it is second-order; this cost is reported.
  • Adam 1e-3, global-norm clip 1.0, batch 8 tasks per update from one family and dimension group, 2,000 updates, final weights. There is no checkpoint selection and no tuning on any split.
  • NaN guard: a skipped update is counted and reported.
  • Seeds and cost. Five training seeds (0–4) per trained arm.
  • The cost estimate is in the infrastructure report.
  • A 20-update timing probe runs first. It may only lower the update count, uniformly for all arms, and only if one run would exceed 45 minutes.

5. Tasks and splits¶

Hashes are recorded. Test tasks are built only after training. Everything uses the make_task generator.

  • Training. Families: isotropic, diagonal κ ∈ {10, 100, 1000}, rotated κ ∈ {10, 100, 1000}, Rastrigin. Dimensions {4, 8, 12}, 4 tasks per family and dimension, seeds 7000+ (96 tasks). Objective scale cycles 0.2/1/5 and coordinate scale 0.5/1/2.
  • Validation seeds 8000+. Used only for the d = 32 ceiling. There is no tuning and no selection.
  • Final test. Seeds 9000+, unseen dimensions {7, 16, 32}, 4 tasks per family and dimension (84 tasks).
  • Curvature domain (primary): diagonal κ = 1000, rotated κ = 100, rotated κ = 1000.
  • Equal-spectrum pairs: the same seed gives the same permuted spectrum for diagonal and rotated κ = 1000.
  • Context: isotropic.
  • Reliability-guard families: Rastrigin (in-distribution), and Ackley and shifted Rosenbrock (held out).
  • Shifted Rosenbrock (rosenbrock-shifted) replaces v0.6's seed-independent Rosenbrock. Each replicate has a seeded shift in [−0.5, 0.5]ᵈ, giving a distinct interior minimizer c(1 + shift). This is tested.
  • Budgets. Primary 100 objective+gradient units; secondary 50 and 200.

7. Metric, statistics and decision rules¶

  • Per task: D = log10(r_cand) − log10(r_ref), with r = max(f − f, 0)/|f₀ − f| and log10 r floored at −12. Raw values are reported as secondary. Negative favours the candidate.
  • Intervals. Every contrast gets a 95% paired bootstrap interval with 10,000 draws.
  • Trained arm versus a reference: two-level crossed resampling of training seeds and tasks.
  • Reference versus reference: resampling over tasks only.
  • Five seeds make the seed level coarse; this is stated in the report.
  • Decision rules (all on the 95% paired interval):
  • Superiority: the whole interval is below 0.
  • Non-inferiority at margin m: the upper bound is below +m.
  • Equivalence: the whole interval lies within ±0.5 decades.

8. Hypotheses and stop rules¶

Stage 1 (gram-scalar and gram-nohist only)¶

  • S1 (non-inferiority): gram-scalar − BB on the curvature domain at budget 100.
  • STOP before Stage 2 only if the upper bound is above +0.25 decades.
  • Reason (recorded): the oracle shows that the headroom over BB comes from the metric (A, C), not the scalar step. A tie at Stage 1 therefore should not end the round.
  • S1b (diagnostic, no stop): gram-scalar − gram-nohist, which measures what the secant τ adds beyond rejection information. Also gram-nohist − BB.

Stage 2 (only if S1 passes)¶

  • Primary, H1: gram-full beats BB on the curvature domain at budget 100, meaning the whole interval is below 0.
  • Reliability guard (G): H1 is void if, on any of Rastrigin, Ackley or shifted Rosenbrock, gram-full − BB has a mean above +0.5 with a lower bound above 0.
  • Secondary, H2 (rotation equivalence): for gram-full, the equal-spectrum difference rotated κ1000 − diagonal κ1000 (paired by seed) lies within ±0.5 decades over its whole interval. The same contrast is reported for BB, the oracle, L-BFGS-B and tf-h8 as context.
  • Secondary, H3 (oracle non-inferiority): the upper bound of gram-full − gram-oracle-BFGS on the curvature domain is below +0.5.
  • "Relearned L-BFGS" criterion (R, pre-stated, evaluated only if H3 holds). R is met if both hold:
  • (i) |gram-full − oracle| ≤ 0.25 decades on at least 75% of curvature-domain task × seed pairs;
  • (ii) on 200 fixed probe states (sampled from oracle trajectories on test tasks, seed 0), the median cosine between the model's and the oracle's step directions is at least 0.99.
  • Exploratory: budgets 50 and 200; per-family and per-dimension results; all arms against L-BFGS-B.

Outcome-to-claim table¶

Claims are cumulative within a row. "No claim" means the result is reported but nothing is asserted. Under no outcome is a claim made of superiority over SciPy L-BFGS-B, or of novelty over L-SR1, Optimus, LODO or the v0.7 literature.

Stage 1 S1 H1 Guard G H3 R H2 What we may claim
fails (upper > +0.25) — — — — — The learned scalar step is worse than BB beyond the 0.25-decade margin. The round ends; BB stays the bar; no Stage 2.
passes fails any any any any Learned geometry did not beat BB on the curvature domain. BB stays the bar. H2 and H3 are reported as exploratory, with no claim.
passes passes violated any any any No superiority claim: the curvature-domain gain comes with a reliability regression on a multimodal family. Both are reported as exploratory.
passes passes holds fails — passes Beats BB on unseen rotated and ill-conditioned tasks at equal budget, with no measurable rotation penalty (±0.5). It remains short of the oracle-BFGS ceiling.
passes passes holds fails — fails Beats BB at equal budget, short of the oracle ceiling. No rotation-invariance claim; the rotated − diagonal gap is reported.
passes passes holds passes met passes Beats BB with no measurable rotation penalty, by relearning L-BFGS-like directions in this loop: no evidence of a new method.
passes passes holds passes met fails Beats BB by relearning L-BFGS-like directions (no new method). No rotation-invariance claim.
passes passes holds passes not met passes Beats BB with no measurable rotation penalty, and is non-inferior to oracle-BFGS (+0.5) without matching its directions. Whether it differs usefully from L-BFGS is not established.

If the upper bound of gram-full − oracle is below 0 (better than the oracle), that is reported as "improves on L-BFGS directions in this loop". It is still reported against SciPy L-BFGS-B, with no claim over it.

9. Costs reported¶

  • Objective, gradient and HVP counts (HVP is always 0), and units used.
  • Accepted fraction, and the fraction of initial steps accepted unchanged.
  • Cold, warm and batched wall-clock (Track A conventions, float64, single process).
  • Training seconds per run, skipped updates, parameter counts, and checkpoint bytes and sha256.

10. Artifacts¶

  • artifacts/gram-v0.7.0-<arm>-seed<s>.npz (Gram schema v2), with metadata recording split hashes and RNG.
  • results/gram-space-v0.7.0.json.
  • Frozen v0.1–v0.6 artifacts and results are never written.

11. Infrastructure built after freezing (before any training)¶

  • The Gram schema v2 mask thresholds and the history_masked switch, with a schema bump and tests.
  • The Gram training scan (the matched unroll), with a parity test against the compiled engine.
  • train_batched support for model_kind="gram", including the NaN-skip test.
  • An evaluation and reference harness that reuses accept_step, with parity checks and the statistics above. A 20-update timing probe is run; no training.
