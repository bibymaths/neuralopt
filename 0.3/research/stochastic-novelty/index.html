Stochastic learned-geometry research status¶

The research gap under investigation is narrower than “neural stochastic optimization.” Learned optimizers already predict updates and preconditioners; Riemannian Langevin/HMC and L2HMC already adapt sampling geometry. NeuralOpt asks whether a variable-dimensional transformer can transfer episode-frozen geometry across optimization task families while distributional structure regularizes candidate generation.

No novelty claim is established by the current implementation. The existing v0.1 transformer was not trained for Langevin mobility, and smoke results show that its geometry often underperforms identity. Exact divergence and weak-form utilities create a testable path to Fokker–Planck-informed training, but no such training ablation has yet demonstrated improved regret.

v0.3 smoke evidence¶

The 15-instance, three-seed artifact is results/stochastic-smoke-v0.3.0.json. All variants use a nominal objective-call budget of 100 and report gradient/integration counts separately.
