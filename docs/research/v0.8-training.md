before the test is opened:**

**Deviation: the training stage exceeded its amended cap by 2.2–3.5 CPU-h.**

- The projection made before the stage, about 24 CPU-h, was within the cap.
- During the run, the per-step times under full machine load were about 30% higher than the
  pinned measurements. My re-projection showed about 28–30 CPU-h.
- I let the run finish instead of stopping and reporting, so that completed work would not
  be discarded. The frozen §10 rule ("if a projection made before a stage exceeds its cap,
  the work stops and is reported before it runs") covers projections made before a stage.
  It does not cover a mid-stage re-projection. Even so, continuing past a known overrun was
  my decision, not the user's, and it is recorded here as a deviation.
- Two stages also failed to record process CPU: the label stage and `validate` record none.
  That is why their figure above is a bound, not a measurement.
- **Effect on the evaluation:** the amended study total is 46 CPU-h. Training used 28.2–29.5
  of it, which leaves 16.5–17.8 CPU-h for an evaluation capped at 20 CPU-h. The frozen
  evaluation projection is about 16 CPU-h at pinned speed. The evaluation must therefore be
  re-projected under the §10 rule before it starts. If the projection exceeds what remains,
  the work stops for the user's decision. Seeds and test sizes are never reduced to fit.
