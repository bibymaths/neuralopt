# v0.8 claims register

This register decides what v0.8 may claim. The plan is
[`docs/plans/neuralopt-v08-amortized-families.md`](../plans/neuralopt-v08-amortized-families.md).

**Rules:**
- **Status values:** `unverified` | `supported` | `not supported` | `inconclusive`.
- **Every claim starts `unverified`.**
- **When a status changes:** only through the claim's pre-registered test, on its
  stated split, after the study runs.
- **Exploratory results** may be reported next to a claim, but cannot change its status.
- **Freezing the criteria:**
  - The criteria below are the **proposed** decision rules.
  - They are copied verbatim into the v0.8 pre-registration and frozen there (sha256
    recorded) **before any training** (stop point 3).
  - Any change before the freeze is recorded in the history table at the end.
- **Release freeze:**
  - There is no v0.8.0 tag, PyPI upload or GitHub release until every claim has a
    pre-registered result and the user has approved the release.
  - Public text calls a claim "under test" until it is `supported`.

**Shared definitions:**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Arms A0–A5, A2o, A4K, A4F | defined in plan §5 |
| Success | `f(θ̂) − f* ≤ ε_abs + ε_rel·|f*|` (plan §6), with ε fixed per family before F0 |
| Units | objective + gradient calls, all counted, fallback included. **Objective and gradient calls are always reported separately for every arm**, because one JAX/ODE gradient costs several objective calls; the measured gradient/objective cost ratio per family (F0 timing probe) is reported too. |
| Matched budget `B` | per family, fixed in the pre-registration from the F0 report (proposed: `B = 2·c_local`, rounded up to the F0 grid) |
| Intervals | paired over instances, percentile bootstrap, 95%, 10,000 resamples. Learned arms use **≥ 5 predictor training seeds**: each instance's outcome is the mean over seeds; per-seed results and their spread are reported as well. |
| Families | each claim is decided **per family/variant** that passed F0 (1, 2a, 2b), with its criterion applied separately to each. The register lists one status per family. |
| Cost-to-95% | `B95`: the smallest budget on the F0 grid at which an arm reaches 95% success; its interval comes from a bootstrap of the success-versus-budget curve over instances |
| Default split | the in-distribution test split, opened once |

## C1. Basin landing

- **Wording:** Learned start + L-BFGS reaches the reference optimum on more instances than
  default start + L-BFGS at the same budget.
- **Test:** A4 versus A0 at budget `B`, owned L-BFGS in both.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** if the paired success-rate difference A4 − A0
  has a 95% interval whose lower bound is > 0.
  - **Not supported** if the upper bound is ≤ 0.
  - **Inconclusive** otherwise.
- **Split:** in-distribution test.
- **Status:** unverified

## C2. Cost versus multistart

- **Wording:** At a matched success rate, learned start + L-BFGS uses fewer units and less
  wall time than multistart L-BFGS-B.
- **Test:** A4 versus the **better of** SciPy multistart (A2) and owned multistart (A2o).
  - "Better" is decided separately on each axis: the lower `B95` for units, the lower warm
    time for wall-clock.
  - The matched rate is 95% success. Compared: `B95` in units, and warm wall time per
    instance at that budget.
  - Cold and batched times are reported as well. Objective and gradient calls are reported
    separately.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** only if it holds on **both** axes:
  - the 95% interval of `B95_A4 / min(B95_A2, B95_A2o)` lies entirely below 1;
  - the interval of warm time A4 / (the better arm's warm time) lies entirely below 1.
  - **Not supported** if A4 never reaches 95% within 800 units, or if either ratio's
    interval lies entirely above 1.
  - Otherwise **inconclusive**.
- **Split:** in-distribution test.
- **Status:** unverified

## C3. The neural part earns its place

- **Wording:** The learned start beats the nearest-neighbour warm start, on both success
  and cost.
- **Test:** A4 versus A3, both refined with owned L-BFGS.
- **Criterion (proposed), a superiority claim:** **supported** only if A4 is **better**
  than A3 on at least one axis (a paired interval excluding zero, in A4's favour) **and
  non-inferior** on the other.
  - **Success axis:**
    - better: the interval of the success difference A4 − A3 at `B` has lower bound > 0;
    - non-inferior: lower bound ≥ −0.02 (−2 percentage points).
  - **Cost axis:**
    - better: the interval of `B95_A4 / B95_A3` has upper bound < 1;
    - non-inferior: upper bound ≤ 1.02. This is the −2 percentage-point margin expressed
      as a relative cost margin.
  - **Not supported** if neither axis shows A4 better (each interval includes zero or
    favours A3), or if A4 is inferior on either axis.
- **If not supported**, this entry must state plainly: **"a nearest-neighbour lookup does
  as well as (or better than) the learned start on this family."**
- **Split:** in-distribution test.
- **Status:** unverified

## C4. Break-even

- **Wording:** The number of instances after which training plus target-solving cost is
  recovered.
- **Test:** `N* = T_offline / (t_generic − t_amortized)`.
  - `T_offline` is the measured wall time of:
    - simulating the training set;
    - solving all training targets (the reference multistart);
    - training, including compilation.
  - `t` is the mean warm time to success at the matched success rate of C2.
  - `t_generic` is the better of A2 and A2o; `t_amortized` is A4, with the `g_φ` forward
    pass included.
  - Batched and per-instance variants are both reported.
- **Criterion (proposed):**
  - **supported** if `t_generic − t_amortized > 0` with an interval whose lower bound is
    > 0. Then `N*` is reported with its interval and the measurement protocol: hardware,
    float64, timing method.
  - **Not supported** ("no break-even") if the interval's upper bound is ≤ 0.
  - **Inconclusive** otherwise.
- **Split:** in-distribution test (timings); offline costs from the actual runs.
- **Status:** unverified

## C5. Safety: no silently missed optima

- **Wording:** With the confidence check and fallback, the success rate is not worse than
  multistart L-BFGS-B.
- **Test:** A4F (fallback included in its units) versus A2, at the larger of the two arms'
  `B95` budgets.
  - **Silent miss** (definition): the fallback was **not** triggered, **and** the final
    fit is worse than the reference optimum by more than the success tolerance, i.e.
    `f(θ̂) − f* > ε_abs + ε_rel·|f*|`.
  - The silent-miss rate is the share of all instances that are silent misses.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** if both hold:
  - the interval of the success difference A4F − A2 has lower bound ≥ −0.01;
  - the silent-miss rate's interval has upper bound ≤ 0.01.
  - **Not supported** if either bound fails decisively: the success upper bound is
    < −0.01, or the silent-miss lower bound is > 0.01.
  - Otherwise **inconclusive**.
- **Split:** in-distribution test; also reported on OOD (C8).
- **Status:** unverified

## C6. Informed versus random multistart

- **Wording:** K informed starts beat K random multistarts at equal K and budget.
- **Test:** A4K versus A2o at the same K and the same total units.
  - **K = 4 is primary.**
  - K = 1, 2 and 8 are **exploratory**: reported, but they do not decide the claim.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** if, at K = 4, the interval of the success
  difference A4K − A2o has lower bound > 0.
  - **Not supported** if the upper bound is ≤ 0. Otherwise **inconclusive**.
- **Split:** in-distribution test.
- **Status:** unverified

## C7. Objective-based versus regression training (secondary)

- **Wording:** Objective-based training beats regression on families with
  non-identifiable directions.
- **Test:** A4 trained with the objective-based loss versus A4 trained with the regression
  loss: same architecture, data, seeds and budget.
  - Applies only to families/variants that F0's identifiability check marks as having
    non-identified directions (expected: 2b). Fisher-information conditioning above a
    pre-registered threshold, or flat profiles.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** if the success difference (objective − regression)
  at `B` has lower bound > 0.
  - **Not supported** if the upper bound is ≤ 0. Otherwise **inconclusive**.
  - If no family qualifies, the status is **inconclusive (not applicable)**.
- **Split:** in-distribution test.
- **Status:** unverified

## C8. Generalization and OOD detection

- **Wording:** Performance on OOD splits is reported, and the confidence score flags OOD
  instances.
- **Test:**
  1. All arms on each OOD split (noise, parameter range, sampling times, dose), with the
     same metrics as the in-distribution test. This part is reported regardless of
     outcome.
  2. The AUROC of `1 − confidence` for separating OOD from in-distribution test instances.
  3. The detection rate (the share of OOD instances below `τ_c`) at the validation-tuned
     threshold.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** if, on every OOD split:
  - part 1 is fully reported;
  - the AUROC interval has lower bound > 0.5;
  - the detection rate is reported.
  - **Not supported** if any split's AUROC upper bound is ≤ 0.5.
  - Otherwise **inconclusive**.
- **Split:** the OOD test splits, plus the in-distribution test as the negative class.
- **Status:** unverified

## C9. Precision is not required

- **Wording:** Basin landing does not require precise predictions. The relation between
  prediction error and success is reported.
- **Test:**
  - **Prediction error:** the distance from the start to θ* in normalized log-parameter
    coordinates.
  - **Reported:** success as a function of error decile for A4, plus a logistic fit, with
    the NN start (A3) on the same axes.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** if the success rate in A4's highest
  prediction-error quartile is ≥ 0.8 × A4's overall success rate, with the interval of
  the ratio having lower bound ≥ 0.8.
  - **Not supported** if the ratio interval's upper bound is < 0.8.
  - Otherwise **inconclusive**.
- **Split:** in-distribution test.
- **Status:** unverified

## C10. The forward pass is cheap

- **Wording:** The forward pass costs negligible time compared with refinement.
- **Test:** warm and cold `g_φ` time versus the warm and cold owned-L-BFGS refinement time
  per instance, single and batched, measured in the same process.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** if the warm ratio (forward / refinement) has an
  interval whose upper bound is < 0.05.
  - **Cold times** (first call, compilation included) are reported for both the forward
    pass and the refinement, single and batched, but do not decide the claim.
  - **Not supported** if the warm ratio's lower bound is > 0.05. Otherwise
    **inconclusive**.
- **Split:** in-distribution test.
- **Status:** unverified

## Track v0.8-B: learned step policy in its plausible regime

These two claims belong to the parallel track v0.8-B (plan §15). It has its own
pre-registration, frozen before any v0.8-B training, and no implementation starts until
the user approves its plan. Families:
- Rastrigin, Ackley and shifted Rosenbrock (the `task_families` generators);
- Family 1;
- Family 2b.

Budgets are ≤ 100 units, with a pre-registered grid (proposed {20, 50, 100}). Baselines
are matched on units (objective and gradient calls reported separately) and, separately,
on warm wall-clock:
- owned multistart L-BFGS;
- SciPy multistart L-BFGS-B;
- CMA-ES;
- TPE;
- BB (the compiled loop's BB step);
- quasi-random search (scrambled Sobol points).

Each baseline is tuned on validation only.

## C11. Anytime regret

- **Wording:** The learned step policy beats all baselines on anytime regret on multimodal
  families at budgets ≤ 100.
- **Test:**
  - **Anytime regret** (v0.8-B plan §5.1):
    `AUC_i(B) = (1/B) Σ_{t=1..B} log10(min(max(f_best(t) − f*, τ_i), 10·s_i) / s_i)`,
    where `τ_i` is the success tolerance (floor) and `s_i` is the median regret of 64
    fixed Sobol points (normalization). It is 1 before the first value.
  - **Budgets:** B ∈ {20, 50, 100} units.
  - **Comparison:** the policy (≥ 5 training seeds) is paired over instances against
    **each** baseline.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** for a family only if, at every budget, the
  two-sided 95% interval of the policy-minus-baseline difference lies entirely below zero
  for **every** baseline. The comparison is made under unit matching **and** under warm
  wall-clock matching (v0.8-B plan §4).
  - This is an intersection-union test: all 36 comparisons (6 baselines × 3 budgets × 2
    matchings) must hold, so the family-level error is at most each comparison's.
  - The interval is a two-level bootstrap over seeds and instances.
  - Any claim made jointly across families needs Holm–Bonferroni over those families
    (v0.8-B plan §6). Per-family statuses get no correction.
  - **Not supported** if any baseline is better (interval entirely above zero) at any
    budget.
  - Otherwise **inconclusive**.
- **Split:** in-distribution test of each family; OOD reported (C8 conventions).
- **Status:** unverified

## C12. Probability of reaching the global basin

- **Wording:** The learned step policy reaches the global basin with higher probability
  than the baselines.
- **Test:**
  - "Reached the global basin" (v0.8-B plan §5.2): a measurement-only polish (owned
    L-BFGS, ≤ 2,000 units, gtol 1e-8, uncharged) started from the best-so-far point
    converges to within the success tolerance of the global optimum `f*`.
    - The global optimum is known for Rastrigin, Ackley and shifted Rosenbrock, and is the
      reference optimum for Families 1 and 2b.
    - The polish is not charged to the arm.
  - The rate is compared at each pre-registered budget, against each baseline.
- **Criterion (proposed):** **supported** for a family only if, at every budget
  (B ∈ {20, 50, 100}), the two-sided 95% interval of the paired difference in global-basin
  rate (policy − baseline) has lower bound > 0 for **every** baseline, under both unit and
  wall-clock matching. The statistics are the same as for C11.
  - **Not supported** if any baseline is better (upper bound < 0) at any budget.
  - Otherwise **inconclusive**.
- **Split:** in-distribution test of each family; OOD reported.
- **Status:** unverified
