# v0.8 pre-registration: amendments

Dated amendments to the frozen `docs/research/v0.8-preregistration.md` (sha256 in
`docs/research/v0.8-preregistration.sha256`). The frozen file is never edited.

Each amendment states:
- the date;
- whether any training or test data had been seen;
- what changes and why;
- that no criterion is loosened, or else the user's explicit approval.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-23): C2 is decided on a continuous cost to success

- **Data seen:** validation only. No training has run and no test or OOD split has been
  opened.
- **Decided by:** the user.
- **Reason:** on Family 2b the default start sits at 94.5% success at 400 units, so
  "units to 95%" on the coarse grid flips between 400, 800 and "none" when the instances
  are resampled. The power check showed that C2's units axis would be undecidable at any
  test size. C2's wall axis was defined at that same budget.

### Per-instance cost to success

- **Single-start arms (A0, A3, A4):** `c_i` is the unit count at which the arm's
  best-so-far value first meets the success tolerance. The accounting is F0's: an
  objective call per trial, a gradient on acceptance; a SciPy call is 2 units. The run is
  capped at 800 units, and `c_i = ∞` (censored) if it never succeeds within 800.
- **Multistart arms (A2 with full-budget SciPy; A2o owned):** sequential restarts over the
  16 Sobol starts (seed 3), in order.
  - Start k begins at `k·p` and may use `min(p, 800 − k·p)` units. `c_i` is the unit of
    the first success, or ∞.
  - This is the continuous form of F0's "K starts with B/K units each": success within
    budget b equals F0's K = b/p configuration.
  - **The per-start cap `p` was chosen on validation** from {25, 50, 100, 200, 400, 800},
    maximizing the mean area defined next. Frozen values:

    | | Family 1 | Family 2a | Family 2b |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | A2o | 100 | 400 | 400 |
    | A2 (full-budget SciPy) | 100 | 200 | 400 |

### Summary: the area under the success-versus-budget curve

- **Grid:** G = {10, 20, …, 800} units, 80 budgets.
- **Per-instance area:** `a_i = (1/80)·#{b ∈ G : c_i ≤ b}`, in [0, 1]. It is 0 if the
  instance never succeeds, which is how censoring is handled.
- The mean of `a_i` is the arm's average success rate over the budget grid, equivalently
  1 − its restricted mean cost to success divided by 800, on the grid.

### C2, amended

- **Units axis:**
  - for each required bar (A0, A3, and the better multistart arm), take the paired
    differences `d_i = a_i(A4) − a_i(bar)`;
  - A4's `a_i` is the mean over its 5 training seeds;
  - intervals are the pre-registration's §7 rule: paired, 95%, two-level bootstrap,
    10,000 resamples.
- **Wall axis:**
  - per-instance warm wall cost to success `t_i = t_forward + κ_arm·c_i` for A4 (with
    `κ_A4` the owned-L-BFGS warm seconds per unit), and `t_i = κ_bar·c_i` for a bar; ∞ if
    censored;
  - `κ_arm` is each arm's warm seconds per unit, and `t_forward` the `g_φ` forward time.
    Both are medians over 50 validation instances, run one at a time on pinned single
    threads after compilation, and **frozen before the test is opened**.
  - The time grid for comparing with a bar is `{j·T/80 : j = 1..80}`, `T = 800·κ_bar`.
    Per-instance time-area and paired differences are computed as on the units axis.
- **The better multistart arm** is, separately on each axis, whichever of A2 and A2o has
  the higher mean area on the test split. Taking the stronger of the two on test data
  makes the bar harder, never easier.
- **Supported** only if, on **both** axes and for **every** bar, the 95% interval of the
  mean difference has lower bound > 0.
- **Not supported** if, on either axis, any bar's interval has upper bound < 0.
- **Inconclusive** otherwise.
- **Reported only, not deciding:** units to 95% (B95) on the F0 grid with its bootstrap
  interval; objective and gradient calls separately; cold and batched times.

### C4, consequential change

- C4's `t` ("mean warm time to success at the matched success rate of C2") referred to
  the B95 match that no longer decides C2.
- `t` is now each arm's **restricted mean warm time to success**: the mean of
  `min(t_i, T)`, with `T` as in C2's wall axis for that bar.
- The rest of C4, including its criterion, is unchanged.

### Power check for the amended C2 (validation)

- **Proxies:** the paired area differences between real start strategies. The effect is
  0.05, five points of average success over the grid, about 40 units of restricted mean
  cost.
- `results/c2-validation-v0.8.0.json` and `results/power-c2-v0.8.0.json`, written by
  `scripts/c2_validation_v0_8.py` and `scripts/power_c2_v0_8.py`:

| Family | Mean area: A0 / A3 / A2o / A2 | SD of proxy difference (vs A0 / vs better multistart / vs A3) | n required (max) | Frozen test size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.487 / 0.710 / 0.638 / 0.371 | 0.56 / 0.53 / 0.56 | 982 | 7,000 |
| 2a | 0.924 / 0.921 / 0.909 / 0.879 | 0.03 / 0.05 / 0.03 | 8 | 1,200 |
| 2b | 0.838 / 0.803 / 0.828 / 0.810 | 0.23 / 0.24 / 0.23 | **174** | 300 |

- **No test size changes.** The amended C2 is decidable on Family 2b at its frozen 300
  instances.
- The wall axis uses the same per-instance costs scaled by per-unit times, so its spread
  is proxied by the units axis.

### Loosening check

- A4 must still beat every bar, on both axes.
- Censored instances count as failures (area 0).
- The better multistart bar is chosen on test data in its own favour.

**Nothing is loosened.**

## Amendment 2 (2026-11-23): C3's cost axis uses the same continuous measure

- **Data seen:** validation only, before any training.
- **Made by:** the agent, applying amendment 1's reason. **The user may revoke it before
  the test is opened;** C3's cost axis would then revert to the frozen B95 ratio.
- **Reason:** C3's cost axis compares `B95_A4 / B95_A3`. On Family 1, where C3 is
  primary, A3 never reaches 95% within 800 units (validation: 74.5%). `B95_A3` is
  undefined there, so the axis cannot be decided.
- **Change:** C3's cost axis uses the paired per-instance area difference
  `d_i = a_i(A4) − a_i(A3)` from amendment 1.
  - **Better:** the 95% interval's lower bound > 0.
  - **Non-inferior:** lower bound ≥ −0.02. That is 2 points of average success over the
    budget grid, the same size as the success axis's margin.
  - The success axis and the rest of C3 are unchanged.
- **Power** (Family 1, proxy A0 − A3, SD 0.56):
  - better at 5 points: 982 instances;
  - non-inferiority at 2 points: 6,130 instances.
  - Both fit within the frozen 7,000.
- **Loosening check:** the B95 rule could not be decided on Family 1, and the new margin
  matches the success axis. **Nothing is loosened.**

- **Decided by:** the user.

- **Reason:** training ran about 30% slower under full machine load than the pinned
  measurements. It used 28.2–29.5 CPU-h against its 26 CPU-h cap; that deviation is reported

  | Cap (process CPU) | Before | Amended |
  |---|---|---|

  - **Seeds (5) and test sizes (7,000 / 1,200 / 300; OOD 200 per shift) are unchanged.**

### 4d. How the evaluation reads the frozen text (agent, stated before the test is opened)

Where the frozen text leaves a detail open, the evaluation does the following. None of
these changes a criterion. Each is the direct reading of the text or the project's
existing convention.

1. **Two-level bootstrap (§7):** crossed. Each of the 10,000 draws resamples the 5 seeds
   and the instances independently, with bootstrap seed 0. This is the project's
   convention (v0.6, v0.7, v0.8-B §6). A bar without seeds is broadcast across seed rows.
2. **K starts:**
   - A4K uses the top-K candidates by confidence, with B/K units each.
   - A2o (and A2) at "K starts" uses the first K of F0's 16 Sobol starts (seed 3), with B/K
     units each.
   - Budgets are read off longer runs, F0's convention. C6 is evaluated at B.
3. **A4F at budget b:**
   - If b < B, A4F is A4 within b; the trigger has not been evaluated yet.
   - If b ≥ B, the trigger is evaluated after B units: confidence below `τ_c`, or the refined
     fit at B above `τ_fit`.
     - Not triggered: A4 continues to b.
     - Triggered: A4F's outcome is A4 within B or the fallback. The fallback is A2o with
       K = 2, the first two Sobol starts, (b − B)/2 units each. All units count.
4. **C5:**
   - `b*` is the larger of A4F's and A2's B95 on the F0 grid. An arm that never reaches 95%
     within 800 units uses 800.
   - A2 at budget b is full-budget SciPy multistart in amendment 1's sequential form, with
     its frozen per-start cap p.
   - A4F's B95 comes from its seed-mean success curve.
   - The silent-miss rate is at `b*`: no trigger, and the final fit misses the tolerance.
   - Its exact Clopper–Pearson interval uses x = the sum over instances of the seed-mean
     silent-miss indicator, and n = the number of instances.
5. **C3:** "inferior" on an axis means that axis's interval lies entirely below the −0.02
   margin.
6. **C7:**
   - Evaluated on Family 2b only, the only family §3 flags as non-identified.
   - Families 1 and 2a are "not tested (not applicable: F0 did not flag the family)". The
     objective-loss arm runs only on Family 2b.
   - Seed s of the objective loss is paired with seed s of the regression loss.
7. **C8:**
   - The confidence is the top candidate's confidence, averaged over seeds per instance.
   - The AUROC is of 1 − confidence, with OOD instances positive and in-distribution test
     instances negative. Its interval is a crossed bootstrap over seeds and each class's
     instances.
   - The detection rate is the share of OOD instances whose top confidence is below `τ_c`,
     averaged over seeds.
   - Part 1 reports every arm's success by budget and area on each OOD split.
8. **C9:**
   - The prediction error is the Euclidean distance, in box-normalized coordinates, from
     A4's top start to the reference argmin x*, the lowest point found by the reference
     runs. The number of instances where an arm lowered f* beyond tolerance is reported.
   - Quartiles are per seed. The ratio uses success at B, and its interval is a crossed
     bootstrap.
9. **C10:**
    - The warm ratio is forward time / refinement time for each instance and seed. The
      refinement is owned L-BFGS from A4's top start at budget B.
    - It is measured one instance at a time, in one process, on the first 200 test
      instances, **after all other evaluation work has finished** (run alone).
    - The interval is a crossed bootstrap of the mean ratio.
10. **C4:**
    - `T_offline` for one deployed model is the label stage's wall time, plus the mean wall
      time of the chosen loss's training runs, plus the time to simulate the training set.
    - A3's offline cost is the label stage plus its feature index. N* against A3 therefore
      uses the training run only.
    - `t` is amendment 1's restricted mean warm time, from the frozen κ and forward time.
    - The batched variant uses the C10 stage's batched times and is reported only.
11. **A5** (decides nothing): A4's top start, refined with full-budget SciPy to B.
12. **The reference:**
    - f* is the minimum over the reference and every evaluated final value of every arm,
      learned arms included: the F0 update rule.
    - If two-seed reproduction falls below 99% on a split, the frozen rule doubles that
      split's reference budget. That needs a re-run, so the evaluation stops for the
      user's decision on compute.
13. **Joint statements across families:** none are made, so no Holm–Bonferroni step is
    applied. Per-family results are reported side by side (§7).
14. **The better multistart arm on the wall axis (C2, C4):** A2 and A2o have different
    per-unit times, so each has its own horizon `T = 800·κ`. They are compared on a
    common horizon, `T_c = 800·max(κ_A2, κ_A2o)`.
    - The better arm is the one with the higher mean time-area on that grid, which is
      equivalent to the lower restricted mean warm time to success.
    - A4 is then compared with it on that arm's own grid, as amendment 1 states.
    - On the units axis, the better arm is the one with the higher mean unit area
      (amendment 1).
15. **C4's N\* interval:** when the interval of `t_bar − t_A4` lies above zero,
    `N* = T_offline / (t_bar − t_A4)` is reported with the interval
    `[T_offline / upper, T_offline / lower]`.
    - `T_offline` against A0 and the multistart bar is the full offline cost of one
      deployed model. Against A3 it is the incremental cost: training only, because A3
      shares the label stage.
16. **Reported-only B95 intervals:** a percentile bootstrap of the F0-grid B95 over
    instances: 10,000 draws, seed-mean success for the learned arms, and "none" (above
    800) counted as 1,600 for the quantiles.
