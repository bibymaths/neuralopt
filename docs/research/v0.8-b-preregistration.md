## 3. Expected outcomes (stated before any training)

- **Prior:**
  - v0.4–v0.7 found that learned steps did not beat fixed or BB steps.
  - This study's training budget is about three orders of magnitude below POP's (about
    56 H100-hours).
  - The policy's warm cost per unit (0.27–0.30 ms) is 10–60× that of quasi-random search
    or the owned L-BFGS. So under wall-clock matching the baselines get many more
    evaluations than the policy (§5).
  - **The expected outcome is "not supported" or "inconclusive" in most cells.** A win
    anywhere would be reported for that family only, never as a general result.
- **Rastrigin:**
  - Every baseline reached the global basin on ≤ 1% of validation instances at every
    budget.
  - **C12 is expected to be inconclusive.** A policy would need a basin rate whose
    interval excludes every baseline's near-zero rate.
  - The C11 bar on units is owned multistart L-BFGS: AUC −0.36 / −0.60 / −0.71 at
    20 / 50 / 100.
- **Ackley:**
  - The unit-matched bar is TPE: AUC −0.08 / −0.19 / −0.34; basin rate 0.17 at 100.
  - Under wall matching, TPE gets only 4–13 evaluations, and the bar moves to the
    fastest arms.
- **Family 1:**
  - Owned multistart L-BFGS leads on anytime regret: −0.55 / −0.96 / −1.72.
  - CMA-ES leads on the basin rate: 0.62 / 0.68 / 0.72.
  - C11 is expected not supported or inconclusive.

- **Wall-matched validation (§4.3) sharpens the expectation:**
  - On Family 1, owned multistart L-BFGS with 602 units reaches AUC −3.30 and a basin
    rate of 0.87 at B = 100.
  - On Ackley, quasi-random search with about 1,170 units is the best arm on anytime
    regret at every budget.
  - **A policy that loses any wall-matched comparison cannot support C11 or C12.** Wall
    matching is the most likely deciding axis.

## 4. Arms (frozen)

### 4.1 Policy

- **Architecture** (`PolicyConfig` defaults):
  - history 16; width 32; 4 heads; 2 temporal layers;
  - one coordinate-mixing layer; MLP width 64; head width 32;
  - log-step bound 6; σ in [0.05, 2].
  - Family 1 also gets the instance-context encoder (width 32, 1 layer). The synthetic
    families get no context.
  - Size: 26,274 parameters (37,890 with context).
- **Execution:**
  - the first evaluation is at the shared start point `x0_i`;
  - each new point costs 1 objective call + 1 gradient call (2 units);
  - the policy is run separately at each B ∈ {20, 50, 100}, reading the remaining budget
    fraction;
  - evaluation randomness: `PRNGKey(instance index)`.

## 5. Cost matching (frozen)

- **Units:** objective = 1 unit and gradient = 1 unit, reported separately. Joint calls
  count at their second unit; gradient-free arms make B objective calls (plan §4).
- **Wall clock:**
  - The policy's warm cost per unit is `s_policy`: the median rollout time per instance
    at 100 units, divided by 100. The values are 282.7 µs (Rastrigin), 271.5 µs (Ackley)
    and 298.7 µs (Family 1).
  - The wall budget is `T(B) = s_policy·B`.
  - Baseline k's frozen curve `C_k(t)` is linear with slope `C_k(100)/100`. TPE is the
    exception: it uses the measured median curve, and its slope over units 51–100 beyond
    100.
  - Baseline k gets `B_k = max{t : C_k(t) ≤ T(B)}` units.
  - The wall-matched anytime regret is `(1/T) ∫_0^T L(τ) dτ`, with units mapped to time
    through `C_k`. The basin rate uses the best point within `B_k`.
- **Wall-matched budgets `B_k`** (units, at B = 20 / 50 / 100):

  | Arm | Rastrigin | Ackley | Family 1 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | quasi-random | 236 / 590 / 1,180 | 233 / 583 / 1,167 | 187 / 469 / 938 |
  | owned multistart | 35 / 89 / 179 | 34 / 85 / 171 | 120 / 301 / 602 |
  | SciPy multistart | 30 / 76 / 152 | 25 / 64 / 128 | 28 / 72 / 144 |
  | BB | 22 / 55 / 110 | 21 / 54 / 108 | 25 / 63 / 126 |
  | CMA-ES | 15 / 39 / 79 | 15 / 37 / 75 | 16 / 41 / 83 |
  | TPE | 4 / 10 / 13 | 4 / 6 / 9 | 4 / 10 / 12 |

- **Measured warm cost of one ES generation:** 1,024 episodes × 100 units ≈ 42 s on all
  three families. That is 0.82 ms per episode-step, and the policy forward pass dominates.
- **Actual test timings are recorded.** A deviation above 25% from the frozen curves is
  reported; the frozen curves still decide.

## 6. Metrics (plan §5, frozen)

- **Anytime regret:**
  - `AUC_i(B) = (1/B) Σ_{t=1..B} log10(min(max(f_best(t) − f*_i, τ_i), 10·s_i)/s_i)`;
  - `L = 1` before the first value;
  - `τ_i = 1e-6 + 1e-3·|f*_i|`;
  - `s_i` is the median regret of 64 Sobol points (seed 101).
- **Global basin:** the polish from the best point is NeuralOpt's L-BFGS, ≤ 2,000 units,
  gtol 1e-8, uncharged. The instance is in the global basin if `f(polish) − f* ≤ τ_i`.
- **Optimum values:** `f* = 0` for the synthetic families. Family 1 uses the F0 reference
  protocol, updated if any arm beats it.

## 7. Decision rules (frozen)

- **Paired differences:** per family, paired over the test instances; the policy's
  outcome is the mean over 5 training seeds, and stochastic baselines average 5 run seeds.
- **Intervals:** two-level percentile bootstrap (seeds, then instances), 10,000 resamples,
  seed 0, **95% two-sided per comparison.**
- **"Beats all baselines"** is an intersection-union test over 6 baselines × 3 budgets ×
  2 matchings = **36 comparisons**. All must be significant.
- **C11:**
  - **supported** if every interval of `AUC(policy) − AUC(baseline)` lies entirely below
    0;
  - **not supported** if any lies entirely above 0;
  - **inconclusive** otherwise.
- **C12:** the same rules on the basin-rate difference, with the sign reversed.
  Degenerate cases (all rates 0 or all 1) are inconclusive.
- **Joint claims** across families require Holm–Bonferroni at a family-wise one-sided
  2.5%. Without it there is no general claim.
- **Power:**
  - On the B-S2 validation proxies, the required test sizes are ≤ 7 (AUC, Rastrigin),
    ≤ 1 (AUC, Ackley), 176 (AUC, Family 1) and ≤ 181 (basin, Family 1).
  - **The test size is 200 per family.**

## 8. Training (frozen)

- **ES:**
  - antithetic, with population P = 64 (32 pairs) and noise σ = 0.02;
  - centred-rank fitness, with fitness = −(mean episode AUC);
  - Adam (optax), learning rate 0.01, L2 coefficient 0.005;
  - instances and budgets per generation are common to the population (common random
    numbers);
  - budgets are sampled uniformly from {20, 50, 100};
  - each episode's reward is `−L_i(t)` per unit, summing to `−B·AUC`.
- **Training split only.** The validation checkpoint runs every 25 generations (mean AUC
  over the 3 budgets, unit matching, 200 validation instances), and the best one is kept.
- **Cut order applied to fit the cap** (plan §8), using the measured per-episode-step
  cost:
  - **G = 300 generations, M = 8 instances per member per generation;**
  - projection: about 1.0 CPU-h per run × 15 runs (3 families × 5 seeds) ≈ 15 CPU-h,
    plus validation checks and the test evaluation (about 3 CPU-h) ≈ **18 CPU-h of the
    24 cap**;
  - seeds and test sizes are not reduced.
- **PPO fallback (plan §7.1),** evaluated per family on validation only, after its ES runs
  stop:
  - **Plateau:** a seed has plateaued after at least 200 generations if its best
    validation score improved by less than 0.02 decades over the last 4 checkpoints; or
    when it reaches G = 300. A family has plateaued when at least 3 of its 5 seeds have.
  - **Trigger:** the family has plateaued, and the ES policy does not beat both
    quasi-random search and BB on mean validation AUC at every B, under both matchings.
  - **PPO settings:**
    - the same actor, plus a value head (MLP of width 32 on the mean of the mixed
      coordinate tokens);
    - clip 0.2, GAE λ 0.95, γ 1;
    - learning rate 3e-4, 4 epochs per update, minibatches of 256 steps, 512 episodes
      per update;
    - 5 seeds;
    - its update count is the largest that fits the remaining cap, fixed before PPO
      starts.
  - **Test arm:** exactly one policy arm per family goes to test: ES, or PPO if
    triggered. It is fixed before any test data is opened.
- **Already spent at B-S3** (process CPU): the timing runs and the wall-matched tuning, about 1.2 CPU-h. They count against the cap, so the total projection is about 19.2 of 24 CPU-h.
- **Long-run rule:** tmux, run directories and pinned threads, as in CLAUDE.md rule 9.
- **Caps** (process CPU): 3 CPU-h per process; 24 CPU-h for the whole of v0.8-B (B-S3
  onward).
