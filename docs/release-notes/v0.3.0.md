# NeuralOpt v0.3.0

Released 2026-11-15 (UTC merge date) · PR #3 · [commits](https://github.com/bibymaths/neuralopt/compare/v0.2.0...v0.3.0)

> Opt-in overdamped and underdamped Langevin candidates, Fokker–Planck diagnostics and a periodic function-space reference, with negative smoke evidence for learned geometry. Result type: infrastructure.

This release was tagged retroactively on 2026-11-22; it was not published to PyPI.

## Highlights

- Opt-in overdamped Euler–Maruyama and underdamped BAOAB candidate trajectories with
  identity or learned diagonal-plus-low-rank geometry.
- Exact small-dimensional mobility divergence and weak Fokker–Planck diagnostics.
- A periodic Gaussian function-space reference that refines consistently across four
  resolutions.

## Features

- Overdamped Langevin (Euler–Maruyama) and BAOAB integrators with episode-frozen
  geometry, explicit PRNG keys and the same logistic box transform as HMC.
- `mobility_divergence`: exact Itô divergence term via `jacfwd`, for small dimensions.
- Weak-form Fokker–Planck generator residual and an analytic Gaussian free-energy
  reference.
- Periodic Gaussian reference measure with mesh-normalized Fourier reconstruction at
  32, 64, 128 and 256 points.
- Objective, gradient, integration and timing costs reported separately.

## Research results

Infrastructure; the smoke evidence for learned stochastic geometry is negative.

- 15 instances, three seeds, nominal budget 100 (`results/stochastic-smoke-v0.3.0.json`).
- Overdamped identity and learned mobility beat direct on only 5/15 and 4/15 pairs.
  Identity and learned BAOAB each beat direct on 11/15.
- Learned geometry has not established a consistent advantage over identity.
- Gates: distribution-aware training and function-space transfer are partial;
  certification injection is blocked. Certification lower bounds are unchanged.

## Breaking changes

None.

## Known limitations

- Euler–Maruyama and BAOAB have finite-step bias; history-adaptive geometry has no fixed
  invariant measure. No exact Gibbs sampling is claimed.
- No Langevin-specific checkpoint exists; the v0.1 transformer was not trained for
  mobility.
- The function-space experiment validates a discretized reference problem only; no
  mesh-independent transformer claim is made.
- The smoke study is too small for publication-level intervals, and hyperparameters were
  not tuned on a separate validation set.
- The retroactive release workflow does not rerun the test suite at this tag.
