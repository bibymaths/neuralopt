# NeuralOpt v0.4.0

Released 2026-11-15 (UTC merge date) · PR #4 · [commits](https://github.com/bibymaths/neuralopt/compare/v0.3.0...v0.4.0)

> Trains transformer mobility and mass geometry through differentiable overdamped and BAOAB trajectories; learned BAOAB geometry does not beat a fixed diagonal. Result type: negative.

This release was tagged retroactively on 2026-11-22; it was not published to PyPI.

## Highlights

- Transformer diagonal-plus-low-rank mobility and mass heads trained end to end through
  stochastic trajectories.
- A held-out ablation against identity, fixed-diagonal and untrained geometry with paired
  bootstrap intervals.
- The simple fixed diagonal remains the strongest supported BAOAB result.

## Features

- `stochastic_geometry_loss`: pathwise differentiation through explicit-noise overdamped
  Euler or BAOAB trajectories with episode-frozen geometry.
- Losses: multi-horizon `log1p` regret, a stability penalty, a fixed compute-cost term,
  a weak-generator (Fokker–Planck) term and an optional Gaussian free-energy surrogate
  (disabled in the reported run).
- Ablation arms: identity, fixed diagonal, untrained, regret-trained and
  regret-plus-Fokker–Planck.
- Reproduction script `scripts/run_geometry_training_v0_4.py` (with
  `--reuse-checkpoints`) and checkpoints `artifacts/neuralopt-v0.4.0-*.npz`.

## Research results

Negative: learned BAOAB geometry does not beat the fixed diagonal, and Fokker–Planck
transfer to BAOAB is inconclusive.

- Ten held-out pairs, budget 80; paired mean `log1p(regret)` against identity, negative
  is better (`results/geometry-training-v0.4.0.json`).
- BAOAB fixed diagonal: −0.441 [−0.878, −0.078]. BAOAB regret-trained: −0.028
  [−0.259, 0.207]. BAOAB transferred regret + FP: −0.247 [−0.752, 0.072].
- Overdamped: every learned interval crosses zero against identity.
- Overdamped FP − regret: −0.048 [−0.105, −0.006] on the ten pairs. This is a small
  effect on a small prespecified set, not a general advantage.
- At budget 40, transferred FP geometry was worse for BAOAB at both unseen
  temperatures.

Report: [distribution-aware training](../research/distribution-aware-training.md).

## Fixes

- The weak-generator loss evaluates the Gaussian noise term analytically; sampling it
  added a variance term that rewarded shrinking the mobility.
- BAOAB training samples its initial momentum from `N(0, T M)`, matching inference.
- `fixed_metric_diag` is cast to the problem's floating dtype (an integer array kept an
  integer dtype before).

## Documentation

## Breaking changes

None.

## Known limitations

- Small study: six training tasks, ten primary held-out pairs, one training seed, no
  BBOB or scientific inverse problem.
- The FP weight was not selected on a separate validation sweep.
- Transfer evaluations use four instances; their intervals are descriptive.
- v0.5.0 found that the v0.4 feature map discarded the gradient norm, so the model could
  not adapt to objective scale.
- The retroactive release workflow does not rerun the test suite at this tag.
