v0.6 trajectory-aware curvature: pre-registered result¶

Result¶

This is a negative result. The main finding has two parts:

  • The best learned arm loses to an untrained secant step. A Barzilai–Borwein (BB) step-length rule uses the same loop, history and 100-unit budget, with no training. It beats every learned arm by about one decade of normalized regret. For the 8-pair history transformer on the curvature domain, tf-h8 − BB = +0.92 [+0.59, +1.25] (positive means BB is better).
  • Attention adds nothing over an MLP (H2).

Learned geometry does not beat the validation-tuned fixed step under the pre-registered rule. Nothing here supports a claim that learned curvature geometry is competitive with quasi-Newton methods: L-BFGS-B leads by about six decades. The v0.5 negative result stands, and fixed geometry remains the default.

The study was pre-registered in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-trajectory-curvature-v06-design.md, written before any training; its sha256 is recorded in the results file. Hypotheses are tested on the curvature domain (diagonal κ=1000, rotated κ=100 and rotated κ=1000 at unseen dimensions 7/10/16) at budget 100:

  • H1: history plus a rejection signal helps. Supported. The 8-pair history transformer beat its matched no-history control by 0.54 decades.
  • The no-history control drops both the secant features and the rejection counter.
  • So this shows that the history bundle helps. It does not isolate the value of secant pairs.
  • H2 (attention value): not supported. Cross-coordinate attention added nothing measurable over a no-attention MLP fed the same history features.
  • H3 (beats tuned fixed geometry): not supported, under the 3.5× anisotropy cap.
  • On the curvature domain the learned geometry beat the fixed scalar step by 0.22 decades.
  • It failed the pre-registered reliability guard because it regressed on Rastrigin and Ackley.
  • The pre-registered parameterization caps off-axis anisotropy at about 3.5× (limitation 1). Problems needing 100–1000× therefore did not test what learned geometry could do without that cap.

v0.6 adds one positive, narrower finding: a history of actually evaluated states, together with a rejection signal, carries information a small learned policy can use. Even with it, the policy does not reach an untrained secant step.

What was built (opt-in; legacy defaults unchanged)¶

  • neuralopt.trajectory: a fixed-shape shift buffer of (u, g_u, f) records. Each record is evaluated at the same point, in normalized box coordinates.
  • Only accepted states are stored. Rejected candidates only increment a counter.
  • From the buffer it derives masked secant pairs s = Δu, y = Δg_u, and twelve objective-scale-invariant, coordinate-permutation-equivariant history-v1 features.
  • ModelConfig(feature_set="history-v1", history_length=K): a versioned schema.
  • Checkpoints carry schema = "neuralopt-policy/v0.6-history-v1".
  • Unknown, stripped or inconsistent schemas fail closed.
  • Legacy files are written byte-compatibly, and all v0.1–v0.5 assets reproduce a golden snapshot taken before any change (tests/test_legacy_regression.py).
  • minimize(..., candidate_method="preconditioned"): one loop shared by every arm.
  • Update: u' = clip(u − σ L P ĝ, −1, 1).
  • Hard acceptance; σ halves after a rejection.
  • Objective plus gradient calls are charged to the budget, and a gradient is taken once per accepted state.
  • HVP count is 0.
  • train(..., objective="history"): a lax.scan mirror of that loop. Its trajectory and stored history are tested equal to inference.
  • HMC, Langevin, BAOAB, Fokker–Planck, function-space and certification code is unmodified. Stochastic methods reject history configurations (G5 is gated).

Identifiability (documented and tested)¶

On a quadratic, y = H_u s with H_u = J H_x J, J = diag((hi−lo)/2) (test_secant_is_exact_hessian_action_in_normalized_frame). K pairs identify H_u only on span(S), with rank at most min(K, d).

test_history_cannot_distinguish_hessians_agreeing_on_observed_span builds two Hessians that differ by 50× outside the observed span. Starting from the same initial record, they yield identical features. So no feature here implies full-Hessian recovery.

Negative sᵀy is reported as a diagnostic (test_negative_curvature_is_reported_not_hidden). It is never inverted. The geometry P = diag + UUᵀ stays SPD.

Protocol (as pre-registered)¶

  • Task splits:
  • Train: 21 tasks, dimensions 2/4/8, seeds 1000+.
  • Validation: 14 tasks, dimensions 3/6, seeds 2000+.
  • Final test: 84 tasks, unseen dimensions 7/10/16, seeds 3000+. Test tasks were built only after training and validation tuning.
  • Held-out families: rotated κ=1000, Rosenbrock and Ackley.
  • Seeds and comparator:
  • Three training seeds per arm; 400 Adam updates each; the final weights are used.
  • The fixed comparator is a scalar normalized-gradient step L with P = I. L was selected on validation only (0.03 from {0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1}).
  • Metric: D = log10(r_cand + 1e-10) − log10(r_ref + 1e-10) with r = (f − f*)/|f₀ − f*|. Negative values favour the candidate.
  • Confidence intervals: 95% percentile intervals from a two-level bootstrap with 10,000 draws.
  • Each draw resamples both the three training seeds and the test problems, with replacement and independently (crossed, not nested), then averages the paired differences.
  • When both arms are untrained references (fixed, BB, L-BFGS-B), there is no seed level, so only problems are resampled.
  • With only 3 training seeds, the seed level of the bootstrap captures training variation only coarsely.
  • L-BFGS-B budget: every SciPy objective call, line-search trials included, is charged one objective plus one gradient unit against the same budget.
  • Calls are hard-capped at budget/2, so it never exceeds 100 units at budget 100.
  • It averaged 82.7 units, because L-BFGS-B sometimes stops early, after as few as 3 calls.

Limitations and threats to validity¶

  1. Geometry capacity. The pre-registered parameterization inherited from v0.5 (geometry_bound=10, lowrank_scale=0.5) caps the low-rank term at λmax(UUᵀ) < 0.25. Off-axis anisotropy is therefore at most about 3.5×, while rotated κ=100–1000 needs 100–1000×. History can mainly steer the diagonal and the step length. The H3 curvature-domain test is therefore weaker than the research question implies.
  2. Train/test mismatch. Training used a 24-candidate unroll (budget feature over 50 units), random starts and a 1e-6 regret floor. Evaluation used budgets 50/100/200, centred starts and a 1e-10 floor.
  3. The 50/100 budget mismatch is expected to bias H3 and the BB comparison against the learned arms. The fixed step was tuned at budget 100 and BB has no budget input, while learned arms read a remaining-budget feature stretched to twice the horizon they were trained on.
  4. It does not bias H1 or H2, which compare learned arms that share the same mismatch.
  5. The size of the bias was not measured.
  6. float32 floor. 701 of 6,300 runs reach exactly the optimum in float32, so the 1e-10 floor shapes means, especially for isotropic, Rastrigin and Ackley.
  7. A post-hoc recomputation with a 1e-6 floor (not pre-registered; --tables) leaves the verdict unchanged.
  8. Curvature domain: −0.22 [−0.43, −0.03]. Rastrigin: +1.27 [+0.14, +2.55]. Ackley: +0.58 [+0.14, +1.10].
  9. Rosenbrock replicates. analytic_task ignores the seed for Rosenbrock and starts at the box centre. Its four replicates per dimension differ only in scale, and replicate 3 duplicates replicate 0. Its interval is spuriously narrow and should not be cited.
  10. Global search not tested. Rastrigin and Ackley shifts lie in [−0.5, 0.5]ᵈ and every run starts at the centre, inside the global basin. These families test local convergence and basin retention, not global search.
  11. H1 control and H2 scope.
  12. The K=0 control also removes the rejection counter, so H1 cannot separate secant information from rejection-count information.
  13. H2 tested cross-coordinate attention over history-enriched tokens, not temporal attention over pairs.
  14. Feature-schema weaknesses found in review, left unchanged to preserve the pre-registration and assigned to a future history-v2:
  15. Coordinates that never moved receive diagonal curvature 0, with no coverage mask.
  16. Pair masks (‖s‖∞ > 1e-7, ‖y‖ > 1e-6‖g‖) sit near float32 roundoff.
  17. The legacy scale column intentionally breaks objective-scale invariance of the whole token: Rosenbrock replicates differing only in scale give different learned results.
  18. References are conservative. L-BFGS-B received float32 JAX gradients cast to float64, and every call, including line-search trials, was charged two units.
  19. Statistics. Three training seeds make the seed level of the bootstrap approximate. Win counts pair three seeds against one deterministic reference.

  20. Context arm. The frozen v0.5 checkpoint was trained on Rastrigin and Ackley and for a different loop. It is context only.

  21. Training/inference equivalence coverage. test_training_unroll_matches_inference_loop_rule and test_training_scan_history_equals_inference_history cover ordinary gradient scales only.

    • Below roughly ‖g_u‖ ≈ 1e-7, training normalizes with safe_l2_norm (ε = 1e-8) while inference uses the exact norm and stops at a zero gradient.
    • When the objective decreases but the gradient is nonfinite, inference charges 2 units and training charges 1.
    • These regimes are untested and the two loops may differ there.

Corrections to statements in the frozen design¶

None of these changes the analysis.

  • Descent claim. "P SPD ⇒ descent" holds before clipping. With a non-diagonal P, a clipped step can ascend; hard acceptance rejects it.
  • Inverse claim. "Cannot equal H_u⁻¹ for rotated Hessians in d>3" holds generically, not universally. The binding limit is the capacity cap in limitation 1.
  • Invariance claim. "All are invariant to objective scaling" applies to the twelve history columns, not to the legacy scale column. The quadratic-consistency threshold is not invariant to shifting the objective.
  • Undocumented implementation details. These were implemented before any run but are not spelled out in the design:
  • a 1e-6 Σ‖s‖‖y‖ guard in ρ;
  • a 1e-4 relative cancellation threshold for the quadratic-consistency feature;
  • "effective rank" computed as the participation ratio n² / ‖ŜŜᵀ‖_F².
  • Metric. The pre-registered metric (log10 normalized regret) replaces the log1p(regret) named in the draft experiment protocol.

Decision under the stop rules¶

  • H1 passed. History features may be developed further.
  • H2 failed. No attention module is retained beyond the existing architecture. Temporal attention over pairs needs its own pre-registered round.
  • H3 failed.
  • Fixed geometry stays the default.
  • Stochastic mass/mobility with history (G5) is not started.
  • Function-space and certification work (G6) is not started.

Recommended next round, pre-registered before any training: - a history-v2 schema with coverage masks and noise-aware pair reliability; - a geometry parameterization able to express ≥100× anisotropy; - a training unroll matched to the evaluation budget; - BB, rather than the fixed step, as the bar a learned secant policy must clear.
